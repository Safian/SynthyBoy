## Verdict

The own engine's voice path is built on genuinely solid mathematical foundations in its core building blocks — band-limited additive wavetable mipmaps with 4-point Catmull-Rom interpolation, trapezoidal ZDF/TPT state-variable filters, Kaiser-windowed polyphase resampling, a Hann² COLA Laroche–Dolson phase vocoder, an exact exponential-segment DAHDSR envelope, and a deterministic 32-sample absolute-grid scheduler that keeps output independent of host block splits — yet it leaves large, concrete quality and CPU gains on the table through a handful of mathematical bugs, missed architectural refactorings, and ARMv7 hardware traps. The most consequential defects are a `gSafe <= 0.95f` clamp in `core/filters-serum` that caps cutoff on every Moog/EMS/Acid ladder model at 10.67 kHz at 44.1 kHz (a full octave short of Nyquist), a pre-warp wavetable band limit of `3.2 * sampleRate` that aliases harmonics above the 4x oversampled Nyquist (`2.0 * sampleRate`) before the warp even runs, per-unison-voice 128-tap/32-tap decimation instead of post-sum stereo decimation (doing up to 8x unnecessary FIR work), an external Spectral Mask/Vocode 2048-point FFT recomputed every 32 samples (16x too often), missing smooth wavetable frame ramping across the 32-sample control period, sample/granular mip selection that aliases up to a full octave when pitched up or scanned fast, broken `HOST`-locked LFO timing, and unvectorized/spilling ARMv7 NEON float compares plus per-sample `libm` calls (`pow`, `tan`, `atan`, `expm1`, `fmod`, `sin`/`cos`) and a ~60–80 MB multi-voice working set that drives 3x cold-cache penalties on Cortex-A17. In short, the architecture is sound and clean-room credible, but moving it to reference-grade quality and headroom on MPC Key 61 requires fixing those specific math/signal-chain bugs, adopting ADAA/PolyBLEP for the nonlinear and hard-sync warps, moving decimation after the unison sum, shrinking per-voice filter/library memory, and finishing the ARMv7 NEON bit-pattern compare and bit-exactness trade-off discipline.

## Module-by-Module Deep Dive

### Module 1: Wavetable Oscillator

**Files:** `core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.h`, `core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp`, `core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Mipmap construction (`wavetable_bank.cpp:222-332`)**: Every frame stores 18 mip levels (`Wavetable::kNumLevels = 18`, `[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.h:29]`) with harmonic ceilings `{1024, 512, 256, 181, 128, 91, 64, 45, 32, 23, 16, 11, 8, 6, 4, 3, 2, 1}` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp:229]`): octave steps from 1024 down to 256, then half-octave steps (`~sqrt(2)`) below 256. Each level `m > 0` has table length `size(m) = max(256, next_pow2(8 * harmonics[m]))` capped at `kCycle = 2048` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp:231-234]`), with 4 guard samples (`dst[-1] = dst[n-1]`, `dst[n..n+2] = dst[0..2]`, `[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp:318-321]`). Note that `core/engine-own/README.md:13` is outdated: it says "21 half-octave mip levels stored at 2x their band: the eight 64 frame tables take 20.5 MB", whereas the actual code builds **18 levels stored at >= 8x their highest harmonic (minimum 256 samples)**, totalling `13,896` time floats + `2,048` spectrum floats = `63.78 KB/frame`, or `~4.08 MB` per 64-frame table (`~32.65 MB` for 8 tables; `wavetable_bank.h:20` notes ~29 MB for time data alone).
- **Lookup & interpolation (`wt_osc.h:74-80, 951-969, 1110-1240`)**: 4-point Catmull-Rom cubic Hermite interpolation (`hermite()` / NEON `hermite4()`). Frame interpolation (`wt_osc.h:705-725`) linearly blends two adjacent frames `cA` and `cB`.
- **Phase accumulator (`wt_osc.h:362, 663-666, 758, 1131, 1140`)**: Unsigned 32-bit integer `uint32_t phase[kMaxUnison]`, converted to normalized phase via `(ph >> 8) * 2^-24` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:758,1131]`), stepped by `uint32_t` increments derived from `float curInc[v]` (linearly interpolated `prevInc -> curInc` per sample inside the 32-sample control period).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Oversampled mip storage (`>= 8x` highest harmonic, min 256 samples)**: A 4-point Catmull-Rom interpolator has an `O((f/f_t)^3)` transfer error and creates spectral images around multiples of the table sample rate `N * f_0` attenuated as `~sinc^4` (worst-case sidelobe around `-36` to `-45 dB` at 2x oversampling, improving by ~18–24 dB per doubling of table oversampling). By storing tables at `8x` the highest harmonic (`N >= 8 * K`, and at least 256 samples so even a 1-harmonic sine has `256x` oversampling), the first interpolation image falls at `>= 7 * K * f_0` and is suppressed below `-95 dB` — eliminating the classic "glassy" high-note interpolation noise of 2x-stored tables.
- **Phase precision**: A 32-bit fixed-point accumulator wraps modulo `2^32` with zero drift and `fs / 2^32 ≈ 1.03e-5 Hz` frequency resolution (0.00001 Hz at 44.1 kHz, far below the ~3–5 cent human pitch JND). However, truncating to 24 bits via `ph >> 8` before multiplying by `2^-24` quantizes table phase to `2^-24` cycles (~-144 dB phase noise floor, acoustically transparent), while computing `curInc` in `float` provides 24-bit mantissa precision (~`6e-8` relative = `0.0001` cent, matching the measured 0.010 cent tuning accuracy in `[measured: docs/QUALITY.md:24]` for the reference harness).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Band-limited mipmap wavetables with `>= 4x–8x` table oversampling and cubic Hermite interpolation follow Franck & Välimäki (*"Higher-Order Polynomial Interpolators for Wavetable Synthesis"*, 2012) and Välimäki & Huovilainen (*"Antialiasing Oscillators in Subtractive Synthesis"*, IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, 2007).
- **Where it falls short of best practice**:
  1. **Gibbs taper bug** (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp:300-301]`): Using a raised-cosine spectral taper (Lanczos/Fejér-style windowing) to suppress the ~9% Gibbs overshoot on brickwall band-limited waveforms is good practice, **but** the `if` triggers at `h > top * 4 / 5` (80%) while the cosine formula uses `h - top * 85 / 100` (85%). For `h` in `(0.80 * top, 0.85 * top)`, the argument is negative, so `0.5 + 0.5*cos(negative)` dips below 1.0 at 80% and rises back to 1.0 at 85% before rolling off!
  2. **Hard mip-level switching** (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:672-687]`): Best-practice wavetable synths crossfade adjacent mip levels over a small pitch band (e.g., 20–30% of the half-octave interval) to avoid sudden harmonic pops during portamento, pitch bends, or vibrato.
  3. **Stepped smooth frame scanning** (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:222,705-713]`): Best practice linearly ramps the frame blend weight `wB` across the control period; here `prevFrame` is saved in `update()` (`line 222`) but unused in `render()`, setting `wB0 = wB1 = ff` (`line 712`) so smooth frame scanning steps at `fs / 32 = 1378 Hz`.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Zipper noise on smooth frame modulation** `[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:712]`: Stepping `ff` every 32 samples injects 1378 Hz (at 44.1 kHz) sidebands whenever an LFO or envelope sweeps WT position in smooth mode. (Ironically, stepped mode at `lines 714-725` *does* crossfade `wB0 = tStart` to `wB1 = tEnd` across the 32 samples!)
- **Mip-boundary clicks / brightness steps** `[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:681]`: Crossing `levelFor(ratio)` on a sustained pitch glide drops up to half an octave of top partials instantaneously on a 32-sample boundary.
- **Mip taper ripple** `[code core/engine-own/osc/wavetable_bank.cpp:300-301]`: Non-monotonic harmonic weights between `0.80*top` and `0.85*top` on every mip level `m > 0` with `top > 4`.
- **No aliasing in unwarped playback**: `levelFor(nyq / fmax)` selects `maxHarmonics(m) <= 0.49 * fs / fmax` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:654,672]`), keeping all unwarped partials strictly below Nyquist (no denormals, zero DC on built-in tables, user DC preserved intentionally at `wavetable_bank.cpp:349`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-sample**: In `renderPlain` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:1110-1240]`), 4 time samples of one unison voice are vectorized in NEON via `hermite4`, requiring 4 scalar table base+index loads + `vtrnq_f32` transpose (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:954-961]`) — no divisions, no transcendentals, no branches inside the 4-sample loop.
- **Per-control-period**: `WtOsc::update` caches `laneLevel`/`laneSize` (`wt_osc.h:674-687`) and defers silent oscillators (`deferSilent`, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:334]`).
- **Memory & L1/L2 cache**: Each 64-frame built-in table is ~4.08 MB (`55.58 KB/frame` time + `8.19 KB/frame` spec), exceeding Cortex-A17's shared L2 cache (typically 1–2 MB). For a single stationary frame, the active mip level is only `1–8 KB` (fits cleanly in 32 KB L1D). However, with unison `frameSpread` or rapid frame LFO sweeps across 8 voices, active reads span 64 frames × stride `55.6 KB` = ~3.5 MB of sparse 16-byte cache-line touches (`[hypothesis: perf stat cache-misses on Key 61 while sweeping WT position across 8 voices × 3 unison will show elevated L2 misses vs stationary frame]`). Packing mip levels contiguously *by level* (`level[m][frame][sample]`) rather than *by frame* (`frame[f][level][sample]`) would shrink the active working set for a 64-frame sweep at a typical mid-register mip level (`N = 512`) from 3.5 MB to 128 KB!
- **NEON blockers**: `hermite4` vectorizes across 4 time samples of one voice, forcing 4 scattered memory loads and a 4×4 register transpose every 4 samples; on `wt_osc.h:975,982` (`ffloor4`, `sinCycles4`), raw `vcltq_f32` compiles to scalar VFP spills on ARMv7 GCC (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:341]`).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix smooth frame ramping** (`wt_osc.h:707-713`): Ramp `prevFrameOf[v] -> curFrameOf[v]` across the 32-sample chunk (splitting at an integer frame crossing if `floor(prev) != floor(cur)`). *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 10 Hz LFO on WT position, FFT checking absence of `f0 ± 1378 Hz` zipper sidebands.
2. **Fix Gibbs taper threshold** (`wavetable_bank.cpp:300-301`): Use the same `top * 4 / 5` start in both guard and cosine formula. *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Inspect bin magnitudes of `Wavetable::build` on Sawtooth mip levels `m = 1..10`.
3. **Add mip-level crossfade** (`wt_osc.h:672-687`): Blend `level` and `level + 1` in the top 25% of each half-octave band (on medium/high tiers). *Gain:* Small–Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Spectrogram of a 4-octave slow pitch glide showing continuous roll-off without step drops.
4. **Transpose mip layout to level-major** (`wavetable_bank.cpp:273-280`): Store `level[m][frame][sample]` so frame sweeps touch contiguous L1/L2 memory. *Gain:* Medium (CPU on frame-modulated unison patches). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low` on frame-swept presets + `scripts/compare-renders.sh` bit-identity proof.

### Module 2: Warp Modes

**Files:** `core/engine-own/osc/warp.h`, `core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:113-138, 640-937, 971-1109`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Warp catalog (`warp.h:17-63, 104-234`)**:
  - Phase warps (`warpPhase`): Sync, SyncVar, Bend+, Bend-, Bend±, PWM, Asym+, Asym-, Asym±, Flip, Mirror, Remap, Formant (windowed phase stretch), QuantizePhase, FM (Lin/Exp/Self), PD (Cos/Saw/Pulse/Half).
  - Sample/amplitude warps (`warpSample`): Quantize (bitcrush), RM, AM, Distortion (SoftClip, HardClip, Fold/SinFold, LinearFold, ZeroSquare, Asymmetric, Stomp, Rectify, SoftSat).
  - Odd/Even harmonic separation (`wt_osc.h:783-791`) via evaluating `0.5 * (s(p) ± s(p + 0.5))`.
  - Filter LPF/HPF warps (`wt_osc.h:735-754`): 2-pole cascaded backward-Euler one-pole lowpass/highpass (`alpha = x / (1 + x)`).
- **Oversampling & decimation (`wt_osc.h:113-138, 648-655, 826-937`)**: When `warpNeedsOversampling` is true (`[code core/engine-own/osc/warp.h:78-81]`), runs `os = 4` with a 128-tap Kaiser-windowed sinc FIR (`cutoff = 0.105` of 4x rate, `beta = 7.5`, `[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:126-128]`), or when `tick.lowWarp` is set (tier `low`), `os = 2` with a 32-tap Kaiser-windowed sinc FIR (`cutoff = 0.21` of 2x rate, `beta = 7.5`, `[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:127-129]`). Decimation executes inside `for (int v = 0; v < count; ++v)` via `dot128`/`dot32` (`wt_osc.h:131-138, 849-936`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Which warps create new harmonics**:
  - *Discontinuous / C⁰ phase & amplitude warps* (`Sync`, `SyncVar`, `Flip`, `ZeroSquare`, `Quantize`, `HardClip`, `LinearFold`, `Rectify`, `Formant` at non-zero window edges): Introduce jump discontinuities (`O(1/k)` harmonic tail) or slope discontinuities (`O(1/k^2)` tail). Even at 4x oversampling, a `1/k` spectrum only drops ~12 dB across two octaves, leaving audible inharmonic foldover above ~1–2 kHz fundamentals; at 2x (`lowWarp`), alias suppression for hard sync and hard clip is severely insufficient.
  - *Smooth nonlinearities* (`Bend`, `PWM`, `Asym`, `FM`, `PD`, `SoftClip`, `SinFold`, `SoftSat`): Bandwidth grows roughly linearly with modulation index `I` (Carson's rule for FM: `BW ≈ 2(I + 1) f_m`; Bessel `J_n(I)` decays super-exponentially past `n > I`). 4x oversampling handles moderate modulation indices well, provided the *input* wavetable itself is band-limited below the oversampled Nyquist!
  - *Bilinear/linear warps* (`RM`, `AM`, `Odd/Even`, `Filter LPF/HPF`): RM/AM shift frequencies by `±f_mod` (at most `f_c + f_m`, easily band-limited at 2x); Odd/Even and linear Filter warps create **no** new harmonics at all (and `Odd/Even` / `Filter` correctly return `false` from `warpNeedsOversampling`, `warp.h:78-81`).
- **Pre-warp band-limit bug (`wt_osc.h:672`)**: At 4x oversampling (`fs_os = 4 * sampleRate`), the Nyquist frequency of the oversampled grid is `2.0 * sampleRate`. Line 672 computes `ratio = (tick.lowWarp ? 1.0 : 3.2) * sampleRate / fmax`, requesting wavetable harmonics up to `3.2 * sampleRate`! Every wavetable partial between `2.0 * fs` and `3.2 * fs` aliases immediately inside the 4x signal **before** the warp is even applied, and folds straight into `[0.8 * fs, 2.0 * fs]` (and down into the audible band once mixed by the nonlinear warp). Similarly, `tick.lowWarp ? 1.0 * sampleRate` hits exactly the 2x Nyquist `1.0 * fs` with zero transition margin for the 32-tap decimator (`cutoff = 0.21 * 2*fs = 0.42 * fs`).
- **Decimator filter math**: A Kaiser window with `beta = 7.5` gives ~77 dB stopband attenuation and transition width `Delta_f ≈ (77 - 8) / (2.285 * 2*pi * N)` cycles/sample. For `N = 128` @ 4x (`cutoff = 0.105`): transition width `≈ 0.037` of `4*fs`, so passband runs to `~0.0865 * 4*fs = 0.346 * fs` (~15.3 kHz @ 44.1 kHz, -6 dB at 18.5 kHz) and reaches -77 dB at `0.1235 * 4*fs = 0.494 * fs` — very well-dimensioned for 4x, though slightly steep/wide at 128 taps (a 64-tap halfband cascade or 2-stage polyphase IIR elliptic halfband achieves >80 dB stopband at ~1/4 the multiply count). For `N = 32` @ 2x (`cutoff = 0.21`, `beta = 7.5`): transition width `≈ 0.15` of `2*fs`, meaning passband rolls off starting around `0.27 * fs` (~11.9 kHz) and stopband does not reach -77 dB until `0.57 * fs`, letting aliases in `[0.5 * fs, 0.57 * fs]` fold straight into `[0.43 * fs, 0.5 * fs]` (`19–22 kHz`, and lower if `0.57` exceeds `0.5`).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Linear-phase decimation placement**: Because the decimator is a linear time-invariant (LTI) operator, `D(sum_v g_v * x_v) == sum_v g_v * D(x_v)` (exact in real arithmetic, within `1e-7` in float). Running the FIR decimator per unison voice (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:656,826-937]`) instead of summing oversampled unison voices into stereo L/R and decimating **twice** wastes `(U / 2)x` decimation CPU (up to **8x** at 16 unison voices) and `8x` ring-buffer memory (`16 * 127` floats vs `2 * 127`). Moreover, `dot128`/`dot32` (`wt_osc.h:131-138`) does not fold symmetric taps `(x[i] + x[N-1-i]) * h[i]`, missing another ~1.6–2x multiply reduction, or even better, an HIIR polyphase allpass halfband decimator (Valimaki / Laurent de Soras, *HIIR*, 2005 — 12th-order elliptic IIR halfband = 6 multiplies per output sample vs 128!).
- **Better anti-aliasing methods**:
  - **Hard Sync (`Sync`, `SyncVar`)**: Eli Brandt (*"Hard Sync Without Aliasing"*, ICMC 2001) and Välimäki & Huovilainen (IEEE SPM 2007) — minBLEP or 2-point PolyBLEP + PolyBLAMP at the master wrap discontinuity eliminates the `1/k` discontinuity aliasing directly at 1x or 2x oversampling without needing 8x–16x oversampling.
  - **Nonlinear waveshapers (`HardClip`, `SoftClip`, `LinearFold`, `Rectify`, `SoftSat`)**: Parker, Zavalishin & Le Bivic (*"Reducing the Aliasing of Nonlinear Waveshaping Using Continuous-Time Convolution"*, DAFx 2016) and Bilbao, Esqueda, Parker & Välimäki (*"Antiderivative Antialiasing for Memoryless Nonlinearities"*, IEEE SPL 2017) — 1st-order ADAA `y[n] = (F(x[n]) - F(x[n-1])) / (x[n] - x[n-1])` (with midpoint fallback when `|x[n]-x[n-1]| < eps`) has closed-form antiderivatives for all of these shapes (`HardClip`, `LinearFold`, `Rectify`, `SoftClip`, `atan`) and gives 20–40 dB extra alias suppression at 2x oversampling at negligible cost.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Pre-warp aliasing at 4x (`wt_osc.h:672`)**: `3.2 * sampleRate` exceeds `2.0 * sampleRate` Nyquist.
- **Severe aliasing at tier `low` (`os = 2`, 32-tap FIR)**: Hard sync, linear fold, hard clip, zero-square, quantize, and high-index FM alias audibly on high notes (`[hypothesis: Blackman-Harris FFT of Sync ratio=3.5 and LinearFold at key 84 on tier low will show non-harmonic energy above -25 dB]`).
- **Cross-mod linear upsampling (`wt_osc.h:761-762`)**: External FM/RM/AM modulator `extMod` is linearly interpolated from 1x to 4x/2x (`fk = (j+1)/os`), creating `sinc^2` spectral images at `fs ± f_mod` that fold back through FM/RM, and shifting the modulator phase forward by `(1/os)` samples relative to the carrier.
- **Filter warp (`wt_osc.h:735-754`)**: Uses backward-Euler 1-pole (`x / (1 + x)`), which compresses high-frequency cutoff tuning compared to bilinear/TPT (`g / (1 + g)`), and never flushes denormals in `filterState[v]`.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per oversampled sample (`4 * n` or `2 * n` times per voice!)**:
  - `warp.h` calls full scalar `libm` functions inside the oversampled inner loop: `std::expm1` (`kWarpFmExp`, line 152), `std::fmod` (`kWarpDistLinearFold`, line 204), `std::atan` (`kWarpDistSoftSat`, line 230), `std::sqrt` + div (`kWarpDistStomp`, line 222), `std::round` + div (`kWarpQuantize`, line 174), and `std::sin` (`kWarpDistFold` / `kWarpFmLin` when `!fast`). None use `fastmath` on tier `low`!
  - Decimation: 128 MACs per output sample **per unison voice** (or 32 MACs on `lowWarp`). With 3 unison voices × 8 poly voices on Key 61, that is `24 * 32 = 768` FIR taps/sample (or `24 * 128 = 3072` on medium/high) vs `16 * 32 = 512` (or `2 * 16` with symmetric folding, or `16 * 6 = 96` with elliptic IIR halfband) after summing stereo!
  - `warpedGroup4` (`wt_osc.h:971-1109`) only covers a subset of phase warps (`lanePhaseWarp`), and calls `ffloor4`/`sinCycles4` containing raw `vcltq_f32` (`wt_osc.h:975,982`), causing ARMv7 scalar VFP compare spills (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:341]`).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Move decimation outside the unison loop (post-sum stereo)** + exploit symmetric FIR taps (`wt_osc.h:656, 826-937`). *Gain:* Large (CPU: up to 8x on unison warped patches, 2x even on 2-voice stereo). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low --sections` on `Twin Warp Lead`, `Triple Fold Reese`, and a 7-voice warped patch; float32 null test.
2. **Fix pre-warp band limit (`wt_osc.h:672, 565`)**: Change `3.2` -> `1.8` (4x) and `1.0` -> `0.9` (2x `lowWarp`). *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Aliasing FFT at keys 72–108 on `Bend+`, `PWM`, `FM`.
3. **Add 1st-order ADAA to `HardClip`/`LinearFold`/`Rectify`/`SoftSat` and replace `libm` calls in `warp.h`** (branchless triangle fold instead of `std::fmod`, Padé `atan`, `fastmath::exp` for `expm1`). *Gain:* Large (quality + CPU). *Risk:* Medium (subtle tone refinement on folds/clips). *Proving measurement:* Aliasing FFT sweep at tier `low` + `tier_bench` on `Triple Fold Reese`.
4. **Fix `vcltq_f32` in `ffloor4`/`sinCycles4`** (`wt_osc.h:975,982`) via `neon_cmp.h`. *Gain:* Medium (CPU on Key 61). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* ARMv7 `objdump` verification + Key 61 `tier_bench` on warped presets.

### Module 3: Unison

**Files:** `core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:183-491`, `core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:342-412`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Voice layout (`wt_osc.h:209-438`)**: Up to `kMaxUnison = 16` voices arranged as a center voice (if odd) plus symmetric L/R pairs. Detune distributions (`wt_osc.h:444-471`): linear (0), cubic (1), exponential (2), inverse-cubic (3), seeded random (4), shaped by `powerShape(tt, curve)`. Stack styles (`stackRatioSemis`, `wt_osc.h:472-491`) add octave/fifth/chord intervals per voice index.
- **Phase randomization (`wt_osc.h:187-191`)**: Deterministic per note serial via `rng.uni()`: `phase[v] = toPhase(startPhase + randomPhase * rng.uni())`.
- **Gain & stereo spread laws**:
  - *Vital-style law (`wt_osc.h:240-271, 384`)*: Constant-power sin/cos pan (`sa = sin(a)`, `sb = cos(a)` with `a = pi/4 * (1 + spread)`), `centerWeight` and `sideWeight` controlled by `blend`, normalized by `1 / sqrt(sum of power)` (`norm = 1 / sqrt(2 * sum)`).
  - *Serum-style law (`wt_osc.h:272-305`)*: Empirical lookup tables for center/side weights and spread scaling matched to reference loudness.
- **Library oscillator unison (`lib_osc.cpp:357-410`)**: Separate, simpler linear detune & constant-power pan loop (`uIdx` across `[-1, +1]`), scaled by `1 / sqrt(uCount)` (`lib_osc.cpp:367`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Constant-power panning & uncorrelated RMS normalization**: For `U` mutually detuned oscillators whose phases decorrelate over time, the expected summed power is `sum_v g_v^2`. Dividing by `sqrt(sum_v g_v^2)` keeps perceived loudness constant as unison count `U` increases (for coherent detune=0, peaks sum as `sqrt(U)`, e.g., +9.03 dB for 7 voices as recorded in `[measured: docs/QUALITY.md:85]`).
- **Vital-style center-voice gain anomaly (`wt_osc.h:257-271, 384`)**: For outer voice pairs, one channel gets `sa` and the other `sb`, so `sa^2 + sb^2 = 1` for all `spread in [0, 1]`. For the center voice (`wt_osc.h:384`), the code sets `gainL = gainR = centerWeight * (sa + sb)`. At `spread = 1`, `a = pi/2` so `sa + sb = 1.0`. At `spread = 0`, `a = pi/4` so `sa + sb = sqrt(2) ≈ 1.4142` — boosting the center voice's amplitude by **+3.01 dB** (+6.02 dB power!) relative to outer voices and relative to `spread = 1`, breaking constant-power spread invariance on all odd unison counts!
- **Inconsistency between `WtOsc` and `LibOsc`**: `LibOsc` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:357-410]`) completely ignores `t.law` (Serum law), `t.mode` (cubic/exp/random detune), `t.stack`, and `t.blendShape`.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **SIMD layout**: Standard high-performance software synthesizers (e.g., Välimäki et al., *DAFx*; standard AoSoA voice-lane vectorization) pack 4 unison voices across the 4 SIMD lanes so that phase advance, detune, table lookup, warp, and stereo accumulation run 4 unison voices in lockstep with zero cross-lane transpose (`vtrnq_f32`). `WtOsc` instead loops `for (v = 0; v < count; ++v)` sequentially and vectorizes 4 consecutive time samples of one voice (`wt_osc.h:951-969, 1110-1240`). For `U = 1` (mono/sub), time-vectorization is ideal; for `U >= 3`, lane-vectorizing unison voices eliminates the 4×4 transpose when voices share a warp or plain path, though differing mip levels per stacked voice (e.g., octave stacks) require per-lane `base[4]` pointers (which `hermite4` already supports!).
- **Gain law**: Constant-power panning should keep the center voice at `gainL = gainR = centerWeight * 1.0f` (independent of `spread`), with total power normalization `norm = 1 / sqrt(sum_v (gL_v^2 + gR_v^2) * 0.5)`.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **+3 dB center-voice level bulge at `spread = 0`** (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:257-271,384]`): Audible tonal and loudness shift when modulating unison spread on 3/5/7-voice patches.
- **Unison feature drop on Sample/Granular/Spectral (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:357-410]`)**: Detune mode, stack style, and Serum unison law silently do nothing on `oscN.type = 1..3`.
- **Phase coherence when `randomPhase = 0` and `detune = 0`**: Deterministic phase start produces expected coherent `+10 log10(U)` dB peak (`+8.45 dB` for 7 voices, `+12 dB` for 16), safely guarded by master stage.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-unison-voice cost**: Strictly linear `O(U)` for both plain (`renderPlain`) and warped (`render`) paths, except where `morphGroups` caps spectral morph builds (`wt_osc.h:695`). On MPC Key 61, tier `low` caps unison at 3 (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:103]`, affecting 14 of 112 factory presets; heaviest factory preset has 2×7 = 14 unison voices).
- **Per-control-period caching**: Round 12 (`work/opt-osc`, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:334]`) already cached spread/pan sines, detune offsets, and lane increments across unchanged updates (F5/F6), cutting oscillator update cost cleanly.
- **NEON**: Plain path is vectorized 4 time-samples wide; warped path only vectorizes `lanePhaseWarp` modes and falls back to scalar for FM/PD/Distortion warps, and pays `O(U)` decimators (Module 2).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix center-voice gain formula in Vital-style unison** (`wt_osc.h:257-271, 384`): Replace `sa + sb` with `1.0f` (constant power across `spread`). *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Medium (shifts balance on odd-unison presets with `spread < 1`; verify preset loudness). *Proving measurement:* RMS sweep across `spread = 0..100%` for `U = 3, 5, 7`.
2. **Share unison detune/stack/gain helper between `WtOsc` and `LibOsc`** (`lib_osc.cpp:357-410`). *Gain:* Small–Medium (quality/consistency). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Parameter semantics test verifying `detuneMode`/`stack`/`law` affect Sample/Granular unison.

### Module 4: Spectral Morph & Spectral Oscillator

**Files:** `core/engine-own/osc/morph.h`, `core/engine-own/osc/morph.cpp`, `core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:515-624`, `core/spectral/include/synthyboy/spectral/spectral.h`, `core/spectral/include/synthyboy/spectral/kernels.h`, `core/spectral/src/kernels.cpp`, `core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp`, `core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:521-633, 957-971`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Wavetable Spectral Morph (`morph.cpp`, `wt_osc.h:515-624`)**: Reads pre-stored Fourier coefficients `wt.spec` (`kHarmonics = 1024`), applies one of the spectral morph transforms (Vocode, Formant Scale, Harmonic Stretch, Inharmonic Stretch, Smear, Random Amplitudes, Low Pass, High Pass, Phase Disperse, Shepard Tone, Time Skew) over bins `0..K` (where `K` is the band limit), runs an inverse FFT to produce a 2048-sample cycle every `kMorphRebuild = 256` samples (5.8 ms @ 44.1 kHz; `512` samples = 11.6 ms when `lowMorph`), and crossfades linearly from `morphOld` to `morphNew` over 256 samples (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:698-700]`). Rebuild timing is staggered across 8 chord slots (`wt_osc.h:530-544`, Round 13 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:343]`).
- **Spectral Oscillator (`core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp`, `kernels.cpp`, `lib_osc.cpp`)**: STFT phase vocoder with `kFftSize = 2048` (46.4 ms @ 44.1 kHz), `kHop = 512` (11.6 ms, 75% overlap), Hann analysis window and Hann synthesis window (`Hann^2` COLA sum = 1.5, `kOutputScale = 1/1.5`, `[code core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp:29]`). Uses Laroche–Dolson (1999) peak-region pitch shifting (`shapeFrame`, `spectral.cpp:828-868`), full bilateral identity phase locking (`lockToPeaks`, `spectral.cpp:888-910`, fixed in Round 13 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:343]`), spectral-flux transient phase reset (`Analysis::build`, `spectral.cpp:173-198`), and deferred priming frames with 4-quarter hop start offsets (`0/128/256/384`, `spectral.cpp:624-666`, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:352]`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Spectral Morph crossfade**: Linearly crossfading two periodic waveforms `cA` and `cB` over 256 samples (5.8 ms) is mathematically equivalent to amplitude-modulating the harmonic delta by a linear ramp, spreading morph transition energy over `~170 Hz` — smooth for slow sweeps, though when `lowMorph` sets `rebuildEvery = 512`, `wt_osc.h:698-700` still divides by `len = kMorphRebuild = 256`, completing the crossfade in the first 256 samples and holding `wB = 1.0` for the next 256 (an asymmetric stop-start modulation rhythm!).
- **STFT Phase Vocoder (`N = 2048, H = 512`)**: At 44.1 kHz, frequency resolution is `21.53 Hz/bin` (adequate for fundamentals down to ~80 Hz; below C2 adjacent partials fall within 2–3 bins and smear together), while time resolution is `46.4 ms` window / `11.6 ms` hop. The Hann² window achieves exact Constant Overlap-Add (`sum_{m} w^2(n - m H) = 1.5` for `H = N/4`) and `-31.5 dB` sidelobe suppression per window (`-63 dB` product). Bilateral identity phase locking preserves vertical phase coherence across each spectral peak's region of influence, dramatically reducing the classic "phasiness" / reverberant smearing of basic phase vocoders.
- **Brickwall spectral filtering (`spectral.cpp:769`)**: When `bandSmooth = false`, zeroing STFT bins discontinuously multiplies the spectrum by a rectangular function, convolving the time-domain frame with a `sinc` that wraps circularly inside the 2048-sample frame (pre-ringing and time-aliasing).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Spectral Oscillator**: Strongly aligned with Laroche & Dolson (*"Improved Phase Vocoder Time-Scale Modification of Audio"*, IEEE TSAP, 1999) and Röbel (*"Transient Detection and Preservation in the Phase Vocoder"*, ICMC, 2003). The deferred priming + 4-quarter hop stagger (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:352-353]`) is a smart real-time engineering adaptation that cut Key 61 p99 by 37% and strike block by 41%.
- **Where it falls short of best practice**:
  1. **External Mask/Vocode FFT rate bug (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:554-569]`)**: Recomputes a 2048-point complex forward FFT + 2048 `std::cos` + 1025 `std::sqrt` **every 32-sample control period** (`if (ringDirty || e >= 0)` is unconditionally true whenever `e >= 0`!), even though `SpectralVoice` only consumes `s.external[e]` once per 512-sample hop — **16x wasted FFT work**!
  2. **2x wasted IFFT in Spectral Morph (`[code core/engine-own/osc/morph.cpp:440-478]`)**: For `fast == false` (`medium`/`high` tiers), runs a full 2048-point complex IFFT instead of the mathematically equivalent `N/2 = 1024`-point real-packed IFFT (`fast == true`) — 2x the work for `~1e-7` rounding difference. Moreover, it always runs a 2048-point IFFT even at high MIDI notes where `K <= 32` (a `max(256, next_pow2(8*K))` IFFT would be 4x–8x faster with identical harmonic content and `>= 8x` table oversampling!).
  3. **Missing Gibbs taper in Morph (`[code core/engine-own/osc/morph.cpp:217-226]`)**: Unlike `wavetable_bank.cpp:300`, `morph.cpp` brickwalls at `K`, leaving ~9% Gibbs peak overshoot on morphed waveforms.
  4. **Empirical sample-rate gain hack (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:126,370-372]`)**: `spectralGain_ = 1.957 * pow(sr / 48000, 1.457)` is a curve-fitted constant rather than analytical COLA/FFT normalization (a fragile surrogate for sample-rate-dependent loudness scaling).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Non-uniform crossfade on `lowMorph`** (`[code core/engine-own/osc/wt_osc.h:698-700]`): Crossfades over 256 samples then stalls for 256 samples instead of crossfading smoothly across the full `rebuildEvery = 512` interval.
- **Circular pre-ringing** when `bandSmooth == false` (`[code core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp:769]`).
- **Transient onset softness on deferred priming**: Documented as 0.5–0.7 dB noise renormalisation error on Key 61 (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:354]`).
- **Note-on strike overruns on Key 61**: Measured at **2.6–3.1 blocks (7.5–9.0 ms)** on `Spectral Vowel Freeze` / `Spectral Gong Pad` / `Spectral Chord Morph` (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:353-354]`), due to 12 frames + 8 shapings + ~3x cold-cache miss multiplier.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-32-sample-period bug (`lib_osc.cpp:554-569`)**: When an external Spectral Mask/Vocode warp is enabled, executes 1× 2048-pt complex FFT + 2048 `std::cos` + 1025 `std::sqrt` every 32 samples (4× per 128-frame block!) on the audio thread!
- **Per-hop (`spectral.cpp`, every 512 samples)**:
  - `Warp::spread` (`[code core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp:427-445]`) and `Warp::vocode*` (`[code core/spectral/src/spectral.cpp:501-527]`) use nested `O(N * width)` loops instead of an `O(N)` sliding/prefix sum.
  - `Warp::detune` (`360-369`) calls `std::sin`, `std::floor`, `std::exp2` 1024 times per hop; `Warp::shepard*` (`370-382`) calls `std::log2`, `std::round`, `std::exp` 1024 times per hop.
  - `kernels.cpp` still contains raw NEON float compares (`[code core/spectral/src/kernels.cpp:267,298,310-313,540,677,716]`), causing ARMv7 GCC scalar VFP spills in `sincosN`, `wrapN`, `gainClamp`, `placeRegion` and forcing `findPeaksVector()` off on arm32 (18.7 us vector vs 10.8 us scalar, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:352]`).
- **Per-morph-rebuild (`morph.cpp`, every 256/512 samples)**: Calls `std::pow`+`std::log2` per bin in `kMorphInharmonicStretch` (`morph.cpp:270`), `std::cos`+`std::sin` per bin in `kMorphPhaseDisperse` (`morph.cpp:384`), and `std::log2`+`std::floor` in `kMorphTimeSkew` (`morph.cpp:420-422`). Measured morph build cost on Key 61: `0.139 ms/block` on `Stretched Glass Choir` (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:350]`).
- **Memory**: `spectral::Analysis` takes up to 12 KB/frame/ch (up to 16 s, `[code core/engine-own/README.md:46]`); each `SpectralVoice` holds ~37 KB of ring/FFT/phase buffers × 4 unison × 3 oscs × 36 voices.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix External Mask/Vocode FFT rate** (`lib_osc.cpp:554-569`): Run only once per 512-sample hop (or on-demand when a voice advances a hop), with a precomputed Hann table and real-packed 1024-pt FFT. *Gain:* Large (CPU: ~16x on Ext Mask/Vocode). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `synthyboy_bench --engine own` on Spectral + Ext Mask/Vocode preset.
2. **Replace raw NEON float compares in `core/spectral/src/kernels.cpp`** with `neon_cmp.h` bit-pattern helpers and re-enable `findPeaksVector` on arm32. *Gain:* Medium–Large (CPU on Key 61). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* Key 61 `--micro` on `sincosN`/`findPeaks`/`placeRegion` + `tier_bench` on `Spectral Vowel Freeze`.
3. **Use real-packed IFFT on all tiers + pitch-adaptive IFFT size `max(256, next_pow2(8*K))` in `morph.cpp`**, and fix `len = rebuildEvery` in `wt_osc.h:698`. *Gain:* Medium–Large (CPU + smoother `lowMorph` sweep). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --sections` on `Stretched Glass Choir` and `Shepard Ascent`.
4. **Replace `O(N * width)` loops in `Warp::spread`/`vocode*` with `O(N)` prefix sums** (`spectral.cpp:427-445, 501-527`). *Gain:* Medium (CPU when spread/vocode active). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Micro-benchmark on `shapeFrame` with wide spread.

### Module 5: Sample, Granular & Multisample Oscillators

**Files:** `core/sample-osc/include/synthyboy/sampleosc/sample_data.h`, `sample_voice.h`, `core/sample-osc/src/sample_data.cpp`, `sample_voice.cpp`, `core/granular/include/synthyboy/granular/granular.h`, `core/granular/src/granular.cpp`, `core/multisample/include/synthyboy/multisample/multisample.h`, `core/multisample/src/multisample.cpp:450-832`, `core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.h`, `lib_osc.cpp`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Sample (`sample_data.cpp`, `sample_voice.cpp`)**: Precomputes octave mipmaps via a 101-tap Kaiser-windowed sinc LPF (`fc = 0.42*pi`, `beta = 8.0`, `[code core/sample-osc/src/sample_data.cpp:245-295]`). Resamples with a 512-phase windowed-sinc polyphase filter bank (`SincTables`, `[code core/sample-osc/src/sample_voice.cpp:392-449]`): 4-tap Hann (`low`), 8-tap Blackman (`medium`), 16-tap 4-term Blackman-Harris (`high` + cross-mip blend). Loop crossfade uses equal-power cos/sin (`sample_voice.cpp:688-706`).
- **Granular (`granular.cpp`)**: Up to `kMaxGrains = 48` simultaneous grains per voice, reading from the same octave mipmaps using 4-point Hermite interpolation (`granular.cpp:740`), windowed by a 1024-sample table + linear interpolation + 1/64 edge fade across 10 window shapes (`granular.cpp:32-75`), with overlap normalization `1 / sqrt(max(1, lengthSec * densityHz))` (`granular.cpp:316-318`).
- **Multisample (`multisample.cpp:450-832`, `lib_osc.cpp:972-993`)**: Zone-mapped `LayerVoice` (up to 64 layers per `LibOsc`) wrapping `SampleVoice` + per-zone RBJ TDF-II biquad filter (`multisample.cpp:730-780`) + linear DAHDSR envelope (`multisample.cpp:785-830`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Band-limiting on pitch-up**: Shifting a sample upward by ratio `r > 1` scales all source frequencies by `r`, pushing everything above `fs / (2r)` past Nyquist. To stay strictly alias-free with octave mipmaps (where mip `m` is band-limited to `0.42 * fs / 2^m`), one **must** select `m >= ceil(log2(r / 0.84))` (or at minimum `ceil(log2(r))` if accepting small transition bleed).
  - *Sample bug (`[code core/sample-osc/src/sample_voice.cpp:156,267,917,1167]`)*: On `low` and `medium`, `mipFor` uses `round(log2(r))`: for `1.0 < r < 1.414` (`+0` to `+6` semitones), it picks `m = 0`, aliasing up to half an octave! At `r = 1.415`, it jumps abruptly to `m = 1` (audible dark drop + click during pitch bend). On `high` (`sample_voice.cpp:123-146`), blending `m0 = floor(rho)` and `m0 + 1` leaks up to 50% of the aliased upper octave from `m0`. Worse still, `makePlan` passes only `pitchRatio_` — ignoring `speed_` (`sample.scan`, up to **8x**!) and `rateMod` (FM), causing severe aliasing whenever scan speed > 1.
  - *Granular bug (`[code core/granular/src/granular.cpp:95,173]`)*: `mipFor` uses `floor(log2(rate))` — at `rate = 1.99` (almost +1 octave!), it reads mip 0 completely unfiltered, aliasing everything above `fs / 4`!
- **Polyphase phase quantization (`[code core/sample-osc/src/sample_voice.cpp:43,178]`)**: Picking the nearest-below row of a 512-phase sinc table without linear interpolation between adjacent rows introduces a maximum timing jitter of `Delta_t = 1 / 1024` samples, whose RMS phase-noise floor at frequency `f` is `2*pi*f / (sqrt(12)*1024*fs)` ≈ **-56 dB at 20 kHz** and **-62 dB at 10 kHz** (Julius O. Smith, *Digital Audio Resampling*, 2002). Adding linear interpolation between phase rows `p` and `p + 1` drops this floor below -105 dB at minimal cost.
- **Granular window & overlap gain**: `WindowShape::sinc` (`[code core/granular/src/granular.cpp:43-47]`) takes negative sidelobe values down to `-0.212`, violating `granular.h:36` ("in 0 .. 1") and causing phase inversion at grain edges. Overlap gain `1 / sqrt(max(1, lengthSec * densityHz))` assumes uncorrelated grains of unit-RMS window, ignoring both the `p.maxGrains` cap (making dense clouds quieter than expected when capped) and the ~6 dB RMS variation between rectangular (`mean(w^2) = 1.0`) and Blackman/Gaussian (`mean(w^2) ≈ 0.25–0.30`) windows (Curtis Roads, *Microsound*, 2001).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Resampling**: Smith (*"Bandlimited Interpolation — Introduction and Algorithm"*, 2002) recommends interpolated polyphase windowed sinc (`K >= 8` taps, linear interp between phases, or Farrow cubic/quintic) with `ceil(log2(effectiveStep))` mip selection (or half-octave mipmaps like `Wavetable` to keep high-frequency loss under 0.5 octave instead of 1 full octave!).
- **Loop crossfades**: Equal-power `cos`/`sin` crossfade in `SampleVoice` (`sample_voice.cpp:688-706`) is best practice for uncorrelated signals (and correctly phase-continuous at `lStart + xLen`), but is missing for `reverse` and `pingPong` loops. Granular's loop-grains crossfade (`[code core/granular/src/granular.cpp:485,739]`) reads `g.pos - loopLen`, which reads **negative indices (`0.0f` silence)** whenever `loopLo < xfade` and is completely broken for reverse grains (`step < 0`)!
- **Multisample filter (`[code core/multisample/src/multisample.cpp:741-742]`)**: Cases `1` (`lpf_1p`) and `3` (`hpf_1p`) fall through to 2-pole Butterworth RBJ biquads (`Bristow-Johnson, Audio EQ Cookbook, 2005`) instead of 1-pole 6 dB/oct filters.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Aliasing on pitch-up and scan > 1x** in both Sample (`sample_voice.cpp:156,267,917,1167`) and Granular (`granular.cpp:95,173`).
- **Clicks/dropouts in Granular loop crossfade** when `loopLo == 0` or `step < 0` (`granular.cpp:485,739`).
- **Un-oversampled nonlinear warps**: `LibOsc::postWarp` (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:884-891,918-926]`) and Granular's `p.shapeFn` (`[code core/granular/src/granular.cpp:749-750]`) apply Quantize and all Distortion warps at **1x** with neither oversampling nor ADAA.
- **Multisample zipper noise & denormals**: `LibOsc::render` steps multisample gain once per 32 samples (`[code core/engine-own/osc/lib_osc.cpp:975,991]`) instead of ramping across the chunk; `LayerVoice` TDF-II biquad (`[code core/multisample/src/multisample.cpp:775-780]`) has no denormal flush.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **NEON coverage**: Round 10 & Round 13 N1 (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:331,341]`) brought NEON + bit-pattern compares to Sample (`equalPower4`, `sanitize4`, 4/8/16-tap dot products) and Granular (grain window, Hermite, output clamp), dropping Key 61 medians dramatically (`Frozen Pluck Drone` 154% -> 62%, `Chord Cloud Pad` 108% -> 39%, `Gong Grain Swarm` 80% -> 35%).
- **Remaining hot-loop costs**:
  - Granular calls an indirect function pointer `p.shapeFn` per sample (`[code core/granular/src/granular.cpp:749-750]`) when pointwise warps are active (blocks inlining and vectorization).
  - Multisample `LayerVoice` executes `double` divisions per sample in its linear DAHDSR envelope (`[code core/multisample/src/multisample.cpp:798,810,817]`) instead of precomputing `1.0 / stageSamples`.
  - **Memory**: `LibOsc` embeds 16 `SampleVoice`, 16 `GranularVoice` (×48 grains), 4 `SpectralVoice`, and 64 `LayerVoice` inline per oscillator slot × 36 voices (`~25 MB`, `[code core/engine-own/README.md:45]`), harming L2 locality even when only one type is used.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix mip selection to use `|pitchRatio * speed * rateMod|` and `ceil(log2(step))`** (or half-octave mipmaps / strictly band-limited crossfade) in both `sample_voice.cpp` and `granular.cpp`. *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Low–Medium. *Proving measurement:* Pitch-up (+0..+24 st) and 4x scan-speed aliasing spectrograms.
2. **Fix Granular loop crossfade wrap & negative `sinc` window** (`granular.cpp:43-47, 485, 739`) and include `min(expectedGrains, maxGrains)` + per-window RMS factor in overlap normalization (`granular.cpp:316-318`). *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Loop-boundary click test at `loopLo = 0` (forward & reverse) + loudness parity across all 10 window shapes.
3. **Add linear phase-row interpolation to `SampleVoice`** (medium/high tiers), implement true 1-pole LPF/HPF in `multisample.cpp:741-742`, ramp multisample gain per sample in `lib_osc.cpp:975,991`, and precompute reciprocal envelope slopes in `multisample.cpp:798-817`. *Gain:* Medium (quality) + Small (CPU). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 10 kHz transposed sine SNR test (expect noise floor drop from ~-62 dB to <-100 dB) + 1-pole slope check.
4. **Replace `LibOsc::postWarp` 1x distortions with 1st-order ADAA** and specialize Granular `shapeFn` via template/switch outside the sample loop. *Gain:* Medium (quality + CPU). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Aliasing FFT on Sample + HardClip/LinearFold + `tier_bench` on Granular warp patch.

### Module 6: Sub & Noise Oscillators

**Files:** `core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:24-87, 1353-1445, 1669-1748`, `core/engine-own/dsp/noise_coeff.h`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Sub (`voice.cpp:1353-1415, 1669-1680`)**: Instantiates a full `WtOsc` (`sub`) running 1 voice on built-in band-limited wavetables `{0, 15, 3, 1, 2, 4}` (Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, etc.), with pitch tracking, octave/transpose, phase, and pan.
- **Noise (`voice.cpp:24-87, 1416-1445, 1681-1748`, `noise_coeff.h`)**:
  - 9 synthesized noise colours: White (6-uniform Irwin-Hall approximate Gaussian; `fast` splits 3 `xorshift32` draws into six 16-bit halves, `voice.cpp:31-37`), Pink (Paul Kellet 3-pole economy filter, `41-47`), Brown (leaky integrator `0.98 * s + 0.02 * w`, `48-51`), Blue/Violet (1st/2nd difference of white, `52-60`), Geiger (impulse rate `0.0008`, `61-65`), Vinyl (hiss + pop rate `0.0004`, decay `0.96`, `66-72`), Digital Hash (`6.0f`, `73-76`), Velvet (density `0.015f`, `77-80`), Atmosphere (low-passed noise `0.02f`, `81-84`).
  - Optional Sample playback mode (`noiseUseSample`) using `SampleVoice`.
  - Noise filter (`voice.cpp:1694-1710`): 2-stage cascaded 1-pole (`f[0]`, `f[1]`), LPF (`f[1]`), HPF (`x - f[1]`), BPF (`f[0] - f[1]`), coefficients cached in `noise_coeff.h` (Round 12 F12, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:334]`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Sample-rate invariance violation**: Every recursive filter and Poisson event rate in `voice.cpp:41-84` uses hardcoded 44.1 kHz constants. At 96 kHz, Paul Kellet's pink filter poles (`0.99765, 0.963, 0.57`) shift upward by `2.18x` in Hz (leaving a ~+3 dB bass/mid shelf error), Brown noise's corner shifts by `2.18x`, and Geiger/Vinyl/Velvet click rates more than double in events per second!
- **HPF slope asymmetry (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1704]`)**: Subtracting a 2-pole LPF from the input (`x - f[1]`) yields `1 - H(z)^2 = (1 - H)(1 + H)`, which has only a **single** zero at DC (**-6 dB/oct** high-pass slope) instead of a 2-pole HPF `(1 - H)^2 = x - 2*f[0] + f[1]` (**-12 dB/oct**), leaving unexpected low-end rumble when sweeping `noise.filter` toward HPF.
- **White noise Irwin-Hall RNG (`voice.cpp:31-37`)**: Splitting a 32-bit `xorshift32` word into two 16-bit halves gives fast approximate Gaussian white noise, though the low 16 bits of `xorshift32` have slightly weaker spectral tests (Marsaglia, *Xorshift RNGs*, 2003); summing 6 independent draws (or using xoshiro/PCG) avoids subtle bit-pair correlation.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Pink noise**: Paul Kellet (*"Instrumentation-Grade Pink Noise Filtering"*, music-dsp archive, 1999) explicitly defined the 3-pole economy coefficients for 44.1 kHz; for multi-rate engines, either bilinear-transformed 3-pole shelf filters (`fc` specified in Hz) or Voss–McCartney octave generators preserve the `-3 dB/octave` PSD across 44.1/48/88.2/96 kHz.
- **Sub pan bug (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1390]`)**: `subTick.panRight` is never assigned (stays `0.0f`), so the right channel gain is permanently locked to center while only the left channel responds to `sub.pan`!
- **Tier bug in sample noise (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1433]`)**: `noiseSample` hardcodes `QualityTier::high` (16-tap Blackman-Harris sinc + dual-mip blend) even on tier `low`!

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Broken Sub panning (`voice.cpp:1390`)**: Right channel ignores `sub.pan` and voice-control pan.
- **Noise filter & pitch silently bypassed for colours 4..8 and Sample mode (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1715-1733]`)**: Turning `noise.filter` has zero effect on Geiger, Vinyl, Digital, Velvet, Atmosphere, or Sample noise.
- **Sample-rate-dependent noise timbre/density (`voice.cpp:41-84`)**: Different tone and click density at 48/96 kHz vs 44.1 kHz.
- **Denormals**: `noiseState`, `noiseLp`, and `noiseFilt` (`voice.cpp:43-81, 1694-1706`) have no denormal flushing on non-FTZ platforms (though ARMv7 NEON/shell sets FPSCR.FZ `[measured: docs/MPC.md:347]`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Sample noise CPU waste on Key 61**: Hardcoding `QualityTier::high` (`voice.cpp:1433`) executes 16-tap sinc + 2-mip blending (4x–8x the cost of 4-tap `low`) whenever a preset uses a noise sample (e.g., piano key-noise or breath layers).
- **Sub memory bloat**: `Voice::sub` is a full `WtOsc` sized for `kMaxUnison = 16` voices + 16×127-float decimator rings + morph buffers (`~10+ KB` per voice × 36 voices = `>360 KB`) even though Sub is strictly 1-voice, unwarped, unmorphed!

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Set `subTick.panRight = subTick.pan;`** (`voice.cpp:1390`). *Gain:* Medium (quality/correctness). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Sweep `sub.pan` from -100% to +100% and verify symmetric L/R attenuation.
2. **Pass active `QualityTier` into `noiseSample`** (`voice.cpp:1433`). *Gain:* Medium (CPU on sample-noise presets on Key 61). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low` on a preset with noise sample enabled.
3. **Make Noise colour coefficients sample-rate invariant and route colours 4..8 + Sample through `noise.filter`** (with 2-pole HPF `x - 2*f[0] + f[1]`). *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low–Medium (changes timbre of colours 4–8 if filter != center). *Proving measurement:* PSD comparison of Pink/Brown/Vinyl at 44.1 vs 96 kHz.
4. **Replace `Voice::sub` (`WtOsc`) with a slim 1-voice `SubOsc` struct** (phase + inc + gain + hermite read, ~64 bytes). *Gain:* Small–Medium (cache footprint: saves ~360 KB across 36 voices). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* `sizeof(Voice)` delta + float32 null test on Sub presets.

### Module 7: Voice Loop & Voice Allocation

**Files:** `core/engine-own/own_engine.h`, `core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp`, `core/engine-own/voice/voice.h`, `core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Timing & control grid (`own_engine.cpp:1148-1200, 1460-1583`)**: Host block is segmented at MIDI event timestamps and at absolute `kControlPeriod = 32`-sample grid boundaries (`controlPos`), ensuring block-size-independent rendering (`README.md:23-25`). Parameters are snapshotted once per `process()` call (`own_engine.cpp:1330-1365`).
- **Voice pool & allocation (`own_engine.h:89`, `own_engine.cpp:580-750`)**: `kPool = kMaxVoices + 4 = 36` pre-allocated voices tracked by a 64-bit `activeMask` bitset (`forEachActive`, `own_engine.cpp:602-614`, Round 13 V10). Stealing supports oldest / lowest / highest / quietest priorities, legato, note recall, Kill mode (50 ms fade), and on tier `low`, `ghostKill` (`own_engine.cpp:684-730`) which replaces a dying stolen voice with a zero-cost linear DC ramp from its last output sample across all 4 buses (`Main`, `Bus 1`, `Bus 2`, `Direct`), eliminating the voice-count spike on chord retriggers (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:97]`: 33.3 vs 32.0 voice render calls/block).
- **Per-voice render order (`voice.cpp:1615-1892`)**:
  1. Control tick (`Voice::tick`, every 32 samples): envelopes, LFOs, RLFOs, mod matrix, pitch/tuning, osc/sub/noise/filter/bus setups.
  2. Oscillators 0, 1, 2 (`WtOsc` or `LibOsc`, with 10 ms type-switch crossfade) -> stereo scratch buffers + `oscMono` for cross-mod.
  3. Sub + Noise -> stereo scratch + `extMono`.
  4. Filter 1 (`runFilter(0)`) -> Filter 2 (`runFilter(1)`), routing into 4 stereo buses (`Main`, `Bus 1`, `Bus 2`, `Direct`).
  5. Voice amp envelope (`ampPrev -> ampCur`), declick (`tg == 0` only), and accumulation into engine bus buffers.
- **Voice-count gain (`own_engine.cpp:1584-1655, 1672-1683`)**: Inverse-Simpson effective voice count `n_eff = (sum a_i)^2 / (sum a_i^2)`, target gain `T = n_eff^(-alpha)` with asymmetric dB slew (`3.0 dB/ms` attack down, `0.15 dB/ms` release up), applied as a linear ramp `gRamp` across the 32-sample period.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Inverse-Simpson headroom law**: `n_eff = (sum a_i)^2 / (sum a_i^2)` (Hill number of order 2) smoothly counts the number of perceptually active voices: a single loud voice plus 7 quiet releasing tails gives `n_eff ≈ 1.0`, avoiding the pumping/ducking artifact of raw integer voice counting (`1/sqrt(N_active)`), while `N` equal-amplitude voices give `n_eff = N` and `T = N^(-0.5)` constant-power headroom. The asymmetric 3 dB/ms down / 0.15 dB/ms up slew prevents attack clipping without audible pumping on release.
- **Backward cross-modulation split bug (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1619-1623,1658,1833-1835]`)**: When a host block or MIDI event splits a 32-sample control period into sub-chunks (`t0 > 0, n < 32`), `oscMono` and `extMono` are written starting at index `0` (`oscMono[i][0..n-1]`) instead of `[t0..t0+n-1]` (or a circular buffer), overwriting the beginning of the previous period's buffer so backward cross-modulation (e.g., Osc 0 FM'd by Osc 1/2, or Osc FM'd by Sub/Noise/Filter) reads corrupted/stale samples — violating block-size independence!

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Control-grid independence & bitset voice iteration**: Fixed 32-sample absolute control grid + 64-bit `activeMask` bitset (`__builtin_ctzll`) + `ghostKill` DC fade are exemplary real-time audio practices (Ross Bencina, *"Real-time audio programming 101"*, 2011).
- **Buffer passes & cache locality**: `Voice::render` runs multiple passes over 32-sample stereo scratch buffers (`osc0..2`, `sub`, `noise`, `fIn[0..1]`, `fOut[0..1]`, `bus[0..3]`), which themselves fit easily in L1D (~4 KB total on stack). The real bottleneck is **per-voice persistent state size** (~1.5–2 MB per voice with 4 `FilterImpl` + 3 `LibOsc` + 4 `WtOsc`!), meaning touching 8 active voices streams across ~12–16 MB of RAM — thrashing Cortex-A17's L2 cache on chord strikes (directly confirmed by the measured ~3x cold-cache multiplier in `[measured: docs/MPC.md:354]`).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Backward cross-mod corruption on mid-period splits** (`voice.cpp:1619-1623, 1658, 1833-1835`).
- **Steal click on Bus 1, Bus 2, and Direct (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:302-304,1871-1883]`)**: `declick` tracks and fades only `tg == 0` (Main bus); stealing a voice routed to Bus 1, Bus 2, or Direct skips declicking and clicks! (Whereas `ghostKill` in `own_engine.cpp:684-730` properly fades all 4 buses.)
- **Silent/finished voice handling**: Voices in release finish cleanly when `env[0].done()` (plus tail check) and clear their `activeMask` bit, so finished voices cost zero CPU. However, **inside** an active voice, `runFilter(0)` and `runFilter(1)` execute unconditionally even when `!filtOn[f] && !usedFin[f]` (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1751-1824]`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Wasted passes**:
  - Unconditional `runFilter(0)` / `runFilter(1)` (`voice.cpp:1751-1824`): zeroes buffers, ramps gains, and half-sums zeros when filters are OFF.
  - Global LFOs & Random LFOs (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1159-1178]`): advances all 10 global `lfoPhase` and 4 global `RandomLfo` (including Lorenz/Rössler!) every 32 samples even when `activeMask == 0` and no global mod slot is active (contributing to the idle CPU of 4–7% on Key 61 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:108]`).
  - Voice-count gain (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1596-1640]`): invokes 3 `libm` calls (`std::pow`, `std::log10`, `std::pow`) every 32 samples whenever `n_eff > 1`, even during steady held chords where `n_eff` hasn't changed, and applies `gRamp` via a scalar loop instead of `simd::rampLerp`.
  - Stack allocation: `float extNextRef[4][kControlPeriod]` (512 bytes) is declared and touched on the stack in `Voice::render` even when no filter/ext reference is used.
- **Per-voice memory footprint**: `~180 KB × 4` Serum filters (`~720 KB/voice` at 96 kHz, `[measured: docs/QUALITY.md:127]`) + `2 × 32 KB` Classic comb buffers + `~700 KB` `LibOsc` inline pools + `~40 KB` `WtOsc` = `~1.5+ MB/voice` -> `~55–80 MB` across 36 voices. `prepare_voices` alone takes **95.6 ms** at startup on Key 61 (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:327]`).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Guard `runFilter(f)` when `!filtOn[f] && !usedFin[f]`** (`voice.cpp:1751-1824`). *Gain:* Medium (CPU on 0/1-filter patches). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --sections` (`voice render routing/mix/output`) + `compare-renders.sh`.
2. **Fix backward cross-mod indexing (`[t0..t0+n-1]`) and extend steal `declick` to all 4 buses** (`voice.cpp:302-304, 1619-1623, 1871-1883`). *Gain:* Medium (correctness/quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Block-split null test with Osc 0 FM'd by Osc 1 + voice-steal click test on Bus 1/2/Direct.
3. **Skip idle global LFO/RLFO ticks when `activeMask == 0`** (or fast-forward phase analytically on next note-on) and cache/fastmath `n_eff` voice-count gain + vectorize `gRamp` (`own_engine.cpp:1159-1178, 1596-1683`). *Gain:* Small–Medium (CPU, especially idle & steady chords). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Idle callback average (`docs/MPC.md:122`) + `tier_bench` on 8-note chords.
4. **Pool heavy voice sub-objects (delay lines, `LibOsc` players) rather than embedding 36× all types inline** (see Module 5 & 10). *Gain:* Large (L2 cache hit rate, RAM, `prepare_voices` startup). *Risk:* Medium. *Proving measurement:* Key 61 `prepare_voices` ms, `VmRSS`, and strike-block p99 on `tier_bench`.

### Module 8: Envelopes, LFOs & Random LFOs

**Files:** `core/engine-own/env_lfo/envelope.h`, `core/engine-own/env_lfo/lfo.h`, `core/engine-own/env_lfo/lfo_eval.h`, `core/shapes/src/shape.cpp`, `core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:373-509, 755-984`, `core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1154, 1371-1380`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Envelopes (`envelope.h`, `voice.cpp:890-910`)**: 6 per-voice DAHDSR envelopes evaluated every 32 samples (`0.726 ms` @ 44.1 kHz). Curves use `powerShape(x, curve) = expm1(curve * x) / expm1(curve)` (exact normalized exponential curve; denominator cached via `EnvSettings::fillDens()` in V13). Output of Env 1 is squared (`v * v`, `voice.cpp:900`) for perceptual loudness shaping and linearly interpolated (`ampPrev -> ampCur`) at audio rate.
- **LFOs (`lfo.h`, `lfo_eval.h`, `core/shapes/src/shape.cpp`, `voice.cpp:373-509`)**: 10 per-voice + 10 global LFOs supporting built-in shapes, custom breakpoint curves with per-point modulation (`shape.cpp`), sync modes (`Seconds`, `Tempo`, `Dotted`, `Triplet`, `Keytrack`), `Trigger`/`Sync`/`Envelope`/`Sustain Envelope`/`Loop Point`/`HOST`, swing, stereo phase offset, and 1-pole output smoothing. Tier `low` decimates unread Envs/LFOs/RLFOs by 4× (`4 * dt`, `voice.cpp:914-919`).
- **Random LFOs (`lfo.h:136-219`)**: 4 per-voice + 4 global RLFOs with Perlin/smooth-step noise, Sample & Hold, Sine-interpolated random, and two chaotic ODE attractors: **Lorenz** (`sigma = 10, rho = 28, beta = 8/3`) and **Rössler** (`a = 0.2, b = 0.2, c = 5.7`), integrated with 4 substeps of explicit forward Euler (`lfo.h:182-210`).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Exponential envelope + squared amplitude**: `powerShape` maps `[0, 1] -> [0, 1]` smoothly with controllable concavity; squaring `v^2` turns a linear `0..1` decay into a `-12 dB` midpoint drop (approximating Weber–Fechner perceptual loudness decay without the infinite tail of a pure recursive exponential). However, evaluating at `kControlPeriod = 32` (`0.726 ms` @ 44.1 kHz) means any attack or decay shorter than `0.73 ms` collapses into a single 32-sample linear ramp (or skips stages entirely via `envelope.h:148-154`), smoothing transient clicks below ~700 Hz.
- **Lorenz / Rössler forward Euler stability (`[code core/engine-own/env_lfo/lfo.h:182-210]`)**: The Lorenz system has maximum local Jacobian eigenvalue `lambda_max ≈ -23` to `+15` along its attractor wings; explicit forward Euler requires `|1 + h * lambda| < 1` (`h < 2 / |lambda| ≈ 0.08`, and much smaller `h <= 0.01` for trajectory accuracy, Press et al., *Numerical Recipes*, 2007). At `dt_eff = 0.02 * 8 / 4 = 0.04`, Euler sits dangerously close to the stability boundary — which is why `lfo.h:204` needs an emergency `!std::isfinite` reset! Furthermore, `dt = min(0.02 * max(cycles, 0.001) * 50, 0.02)` clamps `cycles` to `[0.001, 0.02]` per 32-sample tick, meaning at 44.1 kHz (`1378 ticks/s`), Lorenz/Rössler speed is **hard-clamped between 1.38 Hz and 27.6 Hz** — turning the Rate knob below 1.38 Hz or above 27.6 Hz does nothing!
- **`HOST`-locked LFO timing bug (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1371-1378,1154]`)**: Line 1371 computes `globals.songSeconds = globals.songBeats * 60.0 / max(globals.bpm, 1.0)` **before** lines 1373–1377 load `globals.bpm` and `globals.songBeats` from atomics (so `songSeconds` lags by one full host block!), and `controlTick()` (`line 1154`) never advances `globals.songBeats` or `globals.songSeconds` across 32-sample periods inside the block. Consequently, `HOST`-locked LFOs freeze for the entire host block (2.9 ms at 128 frames, 11.6 ms at 512 frames!) and depend directly on host block size!

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **ODE integration**: Standard 2nd-order midpoint (RK2) or 4th-order Runge–Kutta (RK4) with adaptive substepping `steps = max(1, ceil(scaledDt / 0.008))` (Press et al., *Numerical Recipes*, 2007) guarantees stability across arbitrary rates (`0.01 Hz` to `100+ Hz`) without clamps or blow-ups.
- **Tempo-sync phase precision**: `LfoCore::offset`, `LfoCore::age` (`[code core/engine-own/env_lfo/lfo.h:74]`), and `OwnEngine::lfoPhase` (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.h:263]`) are single-precision `float`. Over a 10-minute set (`~600 s`, `~200–1200` bars), accumulating `float` phase increments drifts by several milliseconds relative to DAW transport; storing free/sync LFO phase in `double` (or `uint32_t` fixed-point cycles) eliminates long-run tempo drift at zero measurable cost.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Broken `HOST`-locked LFO stepping & block-size dependence (`own_engine.cpp:1371-1378`)**: Produces audible 344 Hz (128-frame) or 86 Hz (512-frame) staircase steps on HOST-locked modulation.
- **0 ms attack click (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1105]`)**: When attack = 0 ms, `ampPrev` snaps directly to `envStart = 1.0`, clicking if the oscillator starts at non-zero phase (e.g., random phase or cosine/square wave). Even a tiny 0.25–0.5 ms declick ramp avoids hard clicks while preserving punch.
- **Retrigger / legato behaviour**: Legato-inverted envelopes cleanly restart from `env[i].current()` (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:335]`, click-free); stolen notes always reset envelopes to 0 (`voice.cpp:282`) and rely on the 64-sample DC declick (which currently misses Buses 1/2/Direct, Module 7).
- **Sub-millisecond envelope collapse (`envelope.h:148-154`)**: Percussion attacks/decays under 0.73 ms skip directly across stages; acceptable for most pads/leads, audible on ultra-snappy FM kicks/plucks.
- **Clamped Lorenz/Rössler rate range (`lfo.h:182-210`)**: Slow ambient chaotic drifts (<1.38 Hz) are impossible.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per control period (every 32 samples)**: Cheap overall (`envelopes + LFOs` fell another 0.011–0.017 ms in Round 13 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:350]`, aided by `EnvSettings::fillDens()` caching and 4× economy decimation for unread modulators on tier `low`).
- **Per-tick libm calls**: `envelope.h` calls `std::expm1` once per active curved attack/decay/release stage per 32 samples (acceptable; could use `fastmath::exp` on tier `low`), and `lfo_eval.h` / `shape.cpp` evaluates custom breakpoint `powerShape` per active custom LFO per 32 samples.
- **Float vs double**: Control-rate phases should be `double` (runs once per 32 samples — negligible cost even on ARMv7 VFPv4).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix `HOST`-locked LFO `songBeats`/`songSeconds` load order and per-control-tick advance** (`own_engine.cpp:1371-1378, 1154`). *Gain:* Large (correctness / quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Block-size invariance test (`32` vs `512` frames) on a HOST-locked LFO modulating filter cutoff.
2. **Replace Lorenz/Rössler forward Euler + rate clamps with RK2/RK4 and unclamped adaptive substepping** (`lfo.h:182-210`). *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Medium (chaotic output changes; update `Lorenz Garden` expectation). *Proving measurement:* Rate sweep 0.05–50 Hz verifying monotonic modulation spectrum and zero `!isfinite` resets.
3. **Promote LFO phase accumulators (`offset`, `age`, `lfoPhase`) to `double`** (`lfo.h:74`, `own_engine.h:263`). *Gain:* Small–Medium (quality: zero long-run tempo drift). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 10-minute tempo-synced LFO phase drift test against analytical `beats / div`.

### Module 9: Modulation Matrix

**Files:** `core/engine-own/matrix/mod_matrix.h`, `core/engine-own/matrix/mod_matrix.cpp`, `core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:986-1084`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Compilation (`mod_matrix.cpp:180-290`)**: Up to 64 modulation slots are compiled only when `blockDirty` is set. Performs DFS cycle detection (`EdgeList`, `mod_matrix.cpp:201-237`) so feedback loops stay unwired without allocation, splits active slots into pass 1 (`modNN.amount` destinations) and pass 2 (all other destinations), and applies `zeroSkip` (V4: skips slots whose source is zero) and `sameSource` (V9: reuses source lookup across consecutive slots sharing a source).
- **Evaluation (`mod_matrix.cpp:350-445`, `voice.cpp:986-1084`)**: Strictly control-rate (`kControlPeriod = 32` samples = `1378.125 Hz` @ 44.1 kHz). Supports 7 control-domain destination laws (`DestKind`: linear, `sqrtLevel`, `quart`, `log2Seconds`, `log2Hz`, `note`, `volume`), bipolar/unipolar scaling, aux source multiplication, custom per-slot curve, rise/fall 1-pole slew + delay, and stereo polarity inversion (flips right-lane modulation sign).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Control-rate sampling & aliasing**: Updating modulation at `f_c = fs / 32 = 1378 Hz` imposes a modulation Nyquist of `689 Hz`.
  - For **audio-rate modulation sources** (`kSrcOscA/B/C`, `Noise`, `Sub`, `Filter1/2`), `voice.cpp:1013-1019, 1659, 1676, 1741, 1771` simply captures the **last single sample** `buf[n-1]` of each 32-sample chunk with zero anti-aliasing filter! Any oscillator or noise component above 689 Hz folds straight into `[0, 689 Hz]` as random-sounding low-frequency aliasing wobble rather than true audio-rate FM/AM/filter-FM.
  - For **key-tracked LFOs** (`sync == 4`) above ~300–689 Hz, control-rate sampling aliases the LFO waveform itself.
  - For **linearly interpolated destinations** (osc pitch, level, pan, filter cutoff), linear interpolation across 32 samples acts as a first-order hold (`sinc^2(f / f_c)`), placing notches at multiples of 1378 Hz while leaving image sidebands at `k * 1378 ± f_mod` (audible as metallic grunge during fast 1–3 ms pitch envelopes or >100 Hz LFOs).
  - For **non-ramped destinations** (smooth WT frame — Module 1, warp amounts, classic filter res/drive on 8-sample grid, multisample level — Module 5), modulation acts as a zero-order hold (`sinc(f / f_c)`), producing much stronger 1378 Hz staircase images.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Compiled sparse slot evaluation with DFS cycle guard**: Best practice for 64-slot modular routing (minimal branching, zero RT allocation, cache-friendly dense active list).
- **Audio-rate modulation best practice**: Modern Serum-class synths (and `core/engine-own/README.md:38`, which explicitly lists audio-rate matrix evaluation as not yet built) split destinations into **control-rate** (unison detune, LFO rate, macro targets, FX mix) and **audio-rate-capable** (osc pitch/FM, filter cutoff, osc/filter level, warp amount), promoting a slot to an audio-rate pass **only** when both its destination is audio-rate-capable *and* its source is fast (Osc/Sub/Noise/Filter, key-tracked LFO, fast LFO > ~40 Hz, or envelope in a <10 ms stage).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Severe decimation aliasing of audio-rate sources** (`[code core/engine-own/voice/voice.cpp:1659,1676,1741,1771]`): Routing Osc A/B/C, Noise, Sub, or Filter to Filter Cutoff or Pitch produces aliased 0–689 Hz stepped modulation instead of audio-rate FM/filter-FM.
- **1378 Hz zipper noise on non-ramped destinations** (warp amounts, smooth frame, multisample gain).
- **Fast envelope/LFO transient smearing**: 1–5 ms pitch-kick envelopes quantize to 1–7 control-period segments.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Current cost per active slot (every 32 samples)**:
  - `ModMatrix::evaluate` (`[code core/engine-own/matrix/mod_matrix.cpp:382,394-396,407]`) calls `powerShape` (2× `std::expm1` + division!) on every active curved slot and aux curve, and calls `std::exp` on every active slewed slot **every 32 samples**, even though `c.power`, `c.auxPower`, and slew times rarely change! Precomputing `invDen = 1 / expm1(-c.power)` and `slewAlpha = 1 - exp(-dt / slew)` inside `ModMatrix::compile()` removes half the `expm1` calls and all steady-state `exp` calls per period.
  - Round 13 measured a `0.011–0.016 ms` reduction in the mod-matrix section on Key 61 (`[measured: docs/MPC.md:350]`).
- **Estimated cost of a selective audio-rate path**:
  - `[hypothesis: running a tagged audio-rate buffer float arMod[32] per audio-rate destination only for slots marked isAudioRate (source in {Osc, Sub, Noise, Filter, KeytrackLFO, FastEnv} AND dest in {OscPitch, OscLevel, FilterCutoff, WarpAmt}), vectorized 4 samples wide in NEON (8 iterations per 32-sample chunk: 1 load + 1 mul + 1 add = ~12–20 cycles per 4 samples -> ~160 cycles / 0.10 us per 32-sample period = ~0.4 us per 128-frame block per active slot per voice, or ~3.2 us for 8 voices = ~0.11% of the 2.902 ms Key 61 block per active audio-rate slot; zero overhead when 0 audio-rate slots are wired)]`. The main cost is not the matrix multiply-add itself, but **consuming** `arMod[k]` inside `Filter` (`tan()` per sample) or `WtOsc` (`exp2()` per sample) — which can be kept cheap via 1st-order Taylor/polynomial `exp2`/`tan` increments around the control-period base!

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Precompute `invDen` for `powerShape` and slew `exp` coefficients in `ModMatrix::compile()`** (`mod_matrix.cpp:382,394-407`). *Gain:* Small–Medium (CPU). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical up to `1e-7`). *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --sections` (`modulation matrix`) on heavily modulated presets (`Lorenz Garden`, `Wah Pressure Clav`).
2. **Add a tagged audio-rate modulation path for fast sources -> `{OscPitch, FilterCutoff, OscLevel, WarpAmt}`** (and at minimum average/boxcar-filter `oscMono`/`extMono` when routed to control-rate-only destinations). *Gain:* Large (quality for FM/Filter-FM/audio-rate LFO patches). *Risk:* Medium (requires polynomial `exp2`/`tan` in consumer hot loops so CPU stays bounded). *Proving measurement:* Spectrogram of Osc B (440 Hz) or key-tracked LFO (1 kHz) modulating Filter 1 Cutoff + `tier_bench --config low` on an audio-rate Filter-FM patch.

### Module 10: Filters as Called by the Voice

**Files:** `core/engine-own/voice/filter_setup.h`, `core/engine-own/voice/filter_unit.h`, `core/engine-own/voice/classic_filters.h`, `core/filters-serum/include/synthyboy/filters/filter.h`, `core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h`, `core/filters-serum/src/filter_lanes.h`, `core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Dispatch (`filter_unit.h`)**: Each voice owns 2 `FilterUnit` slots (`filter[0]`, `filter[1]`), each holding two `ClassicFilter` instances (`cl[0..1]`, models 0–7) and two `filters::Filter` instances (`left`, `right`, models 8+ = 37 Serum filter types), plus optional Clean Mode (`-24 dB` pre-attenuation / `+24 dB` post-makeup), dry/wet mix, and a runaway guard (`|y| >= 200` or non-finite resets and zeroes the block, `filter_unit.h:256-262`).
- **Classic Filters (`classic_filters.h`, models 0–7)**:
  - *Analog (0), Dirty (1), Digital (3)*: Trapezoidal ZDF/TPT 2-pole SVF (`tpt`, `classic_filters.h:563-575`), updated every 8 samples (`5.5 kHz` @ 44.1 kHz). Digital has a NEON stereo pair kernel (`svfPairLoopDigitalNeon`).
  - *Ladder (2)*: Linearized ZDF 4-pole ladder (`classic_filters.h:630-690`) with post-stage algebraic saturator `sat(y) = y - 0.9 * y^3 / (y^2 + 3)`.
  - *Diode (4)*: 4-stage bidirectional tridiagonal coupled TPT ladder solved via Thomas algorithm (`classic_filters.h:790-865`), with 1-sample-delayed global feedback (`dv[3]`) and 1-sample-delayed per-stage `sat(dv[j])`.
  - *Formant (5)*: 4 parallel Cytomic TPT SVFs vectorized 4-wide in NEON (`classic_filters.h:913-961`) + 1-pole tilt.
  - *Comb (6) & Phaser (7)*: Comb uses linear-interpolated fractional delay line with analytical phase-delay compensation (`classic_filters.h:1016-1030, 1085-1087`); Phaser uses cascaded 1-pole allpasses with 1-sample-delayed global feedback (`fbState`).
- **Serum Filters (`core/filters-serum`, models 8+)**:
  - *Core primitives (`filter_dsp.h`)*: `SVFTPT` (Cytomic TPT SVF with dissipative `s1^2` FAT damping, `101-143`), `Ladder4Pole` (ZDF 4-pole with `softClip(S)` feedback saturation, `147-213`), `DiodeLadder` (4 cascaded TPT 1-poles with **non-ZDF** 1-sample-delayed `s[3]` HPF feedback, `216-260`), `Biquad` (Direct Form I, `262-360`), `DelayLine` (linear interp).
  - *Smoothing (`filter.cpp:141-143, 176-177`)*: 1-pole per-sample smoother in **linear Hz** (`alpha = 180 / sr` -> ~5.5 ms time constant) with `tanM` cache memoizing `tan(pi * fc / fs)`.
  - *NEON (`filter.cpp:203-708`, `filter_lanes.h`)*: `processPairT` vectorizes L+R pairs for Scream, EMS/MG Ladder, Diffusor, Allpasses, Multi SVF, Flanges, and RingMod.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **ZDF / TPT topology**: Cytomic trapezoidal-integrated SVF (`SVFTPT`, Classic Analog/Dirty/Digital/Formant) and ZDF `Ladder4Pole` are unconditionally stable for all finite `g > 0` and positive damping `R > 0`, preserve the exact analog frequency-response shape (modulo bilinear frequency warping, compensated by pre-warping `g = tan(pi * fc / fs)`), and handle fast cutoff modulation cleanly because integrator state `s` represents physical capacitor voltage/charge rather than delayed sample differences (Andy Simper, *Linear Trapezoidal Integrated State Variable Filter*, 2013; Vadim Zavalishin, *The Art of VA Filter Design*, 2018).
- **Critical high-cutoff tuning bug (`gSafe <= 0.95f`)**: Under bilinear pre-warping `g = tan(pi * fc / fs)`, `fc = (fs / pi) * atan(g)`. Clamping `gSafe <= 0.95f` in `Ladder4Pole` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h:168]`), `DiodeLadder` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h:234]`), and `processPairT` EMS/MG Ladder (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:406]`) hard-caps the filter cutoff at `atan(0.95) / pi * fs = 0.2419 * fs` = **10.67 kHz at 44.1 kHz** (11.61 kHz at 48 kHz)! Turning the Cutoff knob from 10.7 kHz to 20 kHz does literally nothing on every MG Low 6/12/18/24, MG Ladder, EMS Ladder, MG Dirty, and Acid Ladder model — losing the entire top octave of brilliance! (By contrast, `SVFTPT` at `filter_dsp.h:108` correctly allows `gSafe <= 50.0f`, reaching `0.4936 * fs = 21.77 kHz`.) Note that `G = g / (1 + g)` stays strictly in `(0, 1)` for *any* `g > 0`, so ZDF `Ladder4Pole` does not need a `0.95` clamp for stability!
- **Non-ZDF stability & tuning limits**:
  - `DiodeLadder` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h:243-248]`) uses 1-sample-delayed `s[3]` in its feedback loop instead of solving the ZDF feedback equation `u = (in - k*S)/(1 + k*G^4)`. Delaying feedback by 1 sample adds phase lag `omega = 2*pi*f/fs` around the loop, shifting the self-oscillation frequency downward and lowering the instability threshold at high cutoff (which may explain why someone originally capped `gSafe <= 0.95f`!).
  - `LowEQ12`/`BandEQ12`/`HighEQ12` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:1202-1235]`) use Direct Form I `Biquad`, which suffers from transient state spikes/clicks under rapid cutoff modulation (unlike TPT/SVF or normalized lattice).
  - `ClassicFilter::ladderRamp` on tier `low` (`classic_filters.h:642-690`) linearly interpolates `invDen` independently from `G` across the 32-sample chunk, breaking the exact algebraic identity `invDen = 1 / (1 + k * G^4)` mid-ramp when cutoff sweeps widely.
- **Saturation & aliasing**: All saturators (`softClip(x) = x*(27+x^2)/(27+9*x^2)`, Classic `sat(y) = y - 0.9*y^3/(y^2+3)`, `tanh`) run at **1x** without oversampling or ADAA; under heavy drive or self-oscillation, 3rd/5th/7th harmonics fold back across Nyquist. Placing saturation inside the feedback path (as `SVFTPT` FAT and `Ladder4Pole` do) self-limits resonance cleanly, whereas `Ladder4Pole` applying `softClip` outside the 4-stage core misses the classic Moog inter-stage `tanh` warmth (D'Angelo & Välimäki, *Generalized Moog Ladder Filter*, IEEE TASLP, 2014).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Cytomic TPT SVF (Simper 2013), ZDF ladder with exact feedback prediction (Zavalishin 2018; Stilson & Smith, *"Analyzing the Moog VCF"*, ICMC 1996), coupled tridiagonal Diode ladder (Fontana & Civolani 2010), and phase-delay-compensated Comb (`classic_filters.h:1016-1030`) are all strong, literature-grounded designs.
- **Where it falls short of best practice**:
  1. **`gSafe <= 0.95f` octave-short cutoff cap** (`filter_dsp.h:168,234`, `filter.cpp:406`).
  2. **Shared `combFeedbackLP_` across 4 parallel comb lines (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:1294,1326]`)**: In `Combs` and `Reverb`, all 4 parallel comb delay lines overwrite the same scalar `combFeedbackLP_` variable on every sample, cross-modulating their low-pass states!
  3. **Linear-Hz 5.5 ms smoother (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:141-143,176-177]`)**: Best practice smooths cutoff in log-frequency (octaves/semitones) so upward and downward sweeps are perceptually symmetric, and snaps when `|cur - target| < eps` so `tanM` caching actually hits instead of recalculating `tan()` asymptotically for hundreds of milliseconds.
  4. **Linear delay interpolation in Comb filters (`classic_filters.h`, `filter_dsp.h`)**: Linear interpolation introduces severe high-frequency damping (`cos(pi*f/fs)` roll-off) that varies with fractional delay (pitch-dependent dullness); allpass or 4-point Hermite interpolation is standard for tuned comb filters (Smith, *Physical Audio Signal Processing*, 2010).
  5. **Per-sample `libm` and dead code**:
     - `FilterUnit::process` (`[code core/engine-own/voice/filter_unit.h:157,190,210,221]`): calls `dbToGain` (`std::pow(10, ...)`) **twice per sample** in Clean Mode even when `prevClean == curClean` (constant)!
     - `DiodeLadder::process` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h:239]`): calls `std::tan(kPi * 30.0f / sampleRate)` **every single sample** for a constant 30 Hz high-pass!
     - `LowEQ12`/`BandEQ12`/`HighEQ12` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:1202,1219,1235]`): calls `setLowShelf`/`setPeakingEQ`/`setHighShelf` (`pow`, `sin`, `cos`, `sqrt`, 5 divisions) **every single sample**!
     - `SVFTPT::process` (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter_dsp.h:109,129]`): computes `const float d = 1.0f / (1 + 2*R*g + g*g);` every sample and immediately discards it via `(void) d;`!

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **10.67 kHz hard ceiling on all Serum Ladder/Acid/Dirty models** (`filter_dsp.h:168,234`, `filter.cpp:406`).
- **Cross-corrupted damping state in `Combs` and `Reverb`** (`filter.cpp:1294,1326`).
- **Transient clicks / instability in Direct Form I `EQ12` under fast modulation** (`filter.cpp:1202-1235`).
- **Asymmetric & smeared fast filter envelopes** from linear-Hz 5.5 ms smoother (`filter.cpp:141-143`).
- **Sample-rate-dependent tilt in Classic Formant (`[code core/engine-own/voice/classic_filters.h:940,973]`)**: `tilt += 0.35f * (y - tilt)` uses a fixed 0.35 coefficient independent of `sampleRate`.
- **8-sample coefficient stepping (`5.5 kHz`) in Classic filters**: Can produce subtle zippering during extreme audio-rate sweeps; Comb (`classic_filters.h:1016-1030`) recomputes trig/complex/`exp` every 8 samples without memoizing unchanged parameters.
- **Un-oversampled nonlinear saturation**: Aliases under high drive/resonance (though bounded by `filter_unit.h:256-262` runaway guard).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Divisions in hot per-sample loops**:
  - `ClassicFilter` SVF (`classic_filters.h:568`): 1–2 scalar divisions per sample per channel (even inside `svfPairLoopDigitalNeon` on ARMv7, which has no hardware NEON `vdivq_f32`!). Hoisting `invDen = 1 / (1 + g*R + g*g)` to the 8-sample sub-block eliminates per-sample division entirely.
  - `ClassicFilter` Ladder (`classic_filters.h:670-677`): 4 scalar divisions per sample per channel (8 per stereo sample!) for `sat(y) = y - 0.9*y^3/(y^2+3)`.
  - `ClassicFilter` Diode (`classic_filters.h:810-852`): 4 divisions per sample (Thomas algorithm) + 7 more on the non-fast path.
  - `SVFTPT` (`filter_dsp.h:109`): 1 completely dead division per sample; plus `softClip` (`filter_dsp.h:35`) = 1 division per call.
- **NEON coverage gaps in `core/filters-serum`**: `processPairT` (`filter.cpp:203-708`) delivered huge Key 61 wins in Round 12 (`Lorenz Garden` filter 1: `0.989 -> 0.330 ms`, `Parallel Dusk Pad` filter 2: `1.153 -> 0.303 ms`, `Ring Mod Gamelan`: `1.337 -> 0.557 ms`, `[measured: docs/MPC.md:336]`), **but** does NOT cover the most common Normal family (`MGLow6/12/18/24`, `Low/High/Band/Peak/Notch`), `AcidLadder`, `MGDirty`, `NewWsp`, `ExpMM`, `Formant*`, or `EQ*` — which still run two sequential scalar channel loops!
- **Memory footprint**: Every `FilterImpl` unconditionally allocates 14 delay lines (`[code core/filters-serum/src/filter.cpp:39-60]`) sized for 96 kHz (~180 KB per filter -> ~25 MB across voice filters, `[measured: docs/QUALITY.md:127]`), even when running a stateless-delay 2-pole SVF! That is the single largest avoidable L2 cache and RAM cost in the voice path.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix `gSafe <= 0.95f` cap** (`filter_dsp.h:168,234`, `filter.cpp:406`) and convert `DiodeLadder` (`filter_dsp.h:228-260`) to ZDF feedback so it stays tuned and stable up to 20 kHz. *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Medium (changes top-octave brightness on Ladder presets). *Proving measurement:* `synthyboy_filter_response` sweep at 12–20 kHz + 100% resonance stability test (`docs/QUALITY.md:67`).
2. **Eliminate per-sample `libm` & dead divisions**: Delete dead `d` in `SVFTPT` (`filter_dsp.h:109,129`); cache `gHp` in `DiodeLadder` (`239`); cache Clean Mode gain in `FilterUnit` (`filter_unit.h:157,190,210,221`); memoize + convert `EQ12` to TPT (`filter.cpp:1202-1235`); hoist `invDen` per 8-sample sub-block in `ClassicFilter` SVF (`classic_filters.h:568`). *Gain:* Large (CPU) + Medium (quality on EQ12). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low --sections` on `Acid Saw Reso`, EQ12, Clean Mode, and Classic SVF presets.
3. **Fix shared `combFeedbackLP_` -> `combFeedbackLP_[4]`** (`filter.cpp:1294,1326`) and fix linear-Hz smoother -> log-Hz with snap threshold (`filter.cpp:141-143,176-177`). *Gain:* Medium (quality + `tanM` cache hit rate). *Risk:* Low–Medium. *Proving measurement:* Comb/Reverb impulse test + 2 ms filter-envelope transient test.
4. **Extend `processPairT` NEON pair kernels to `Normal` (`MGLow*`, `Low/High/Band/Peak/Notch`), `AcidLadder`, `MGDirty`, and `Formant*`** (`filter.cpp:203-708`). *Gain:* Large (CPU on Key 61, mirroring Round 12's 2–3x filter wins). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical if `neon_cmp.h` + scalar ops match). *Proving measurement:* Key 61 `tier_bench --sections` on `Acid Saw Reso`, `Formant Vowel Bass`, `Moog Ladder Bass`.
5. **Pool/lazy-size `FilterImpl` delay lines to active `sampleRate` and only for delay-using models** (`filter.cpp:39-60`). *Gain:* Large (RAM: saves ~20+ MB; CPU: better L2 cache locality & faster `prepare_voices`). *Risk:* Medium. *Proving measurement:* `VmRSS`, `prepare_voices` startup time, and Key 61 strike-block p99.

## What to Watch at the Finest Level

1. Never use raw NEON float compares (`vcltq_f32`/`vcgtq_f32`/`vcgeq_f32`) on ARMv7 GCC without `-funsafe-math-optimizations`; always use `neon_cmp.h` bit-pattern compares (`wt_osc.h:975,982`, `kernels.cpp:267,298,310-313,540,677,716`).
2. Never run a linear decimator inside a per-unison-voice loop: sum oversampled voices to stereo first, then decimate twice (`wt_osc.h:656,826-937`).
3. Keep pre-warp wavetable band limits strictly below the oversampled Nyquist (`< 0.5 * os * fs`, never `3.2 * fs` at 4x, `wt_osc.h:672`).
4. Never clamp bilinear `g = tan(pi * fc / fs)` below `1.0` (`0.95` caps cutoff at `0.2419 * fs` = 10.67 kHz @ 44.1 kHz, `filter_dsp.h:168,234`, `filter.cpp:406`).
5. Never put `std::pow`, `std::tan`, `std::atan`, `std::expm1`, `std::fmod`, `std::sin`, or `std::cos` inside a per-sample or per-oversampled-sample loop (`filter_unit.h:157`, `filter_dsp.h:239`, `filter.cpp:1202`, `warp.h:152,204,230`).
6. Check for dead divisions that survive optimization under `-ffp-contract=off` (`const float d = 1 / ...; (void) d;` in `filter_dsp.h:109,129`).
7. Never recompute a block-rate or hop-rate FFT inside a 32-sample control tick (`lib_osc.cpp:554-569` runs 16x too often).
8. Always use real-packed `N/2` FFT/IFFT for real-valued signals and size morph IFFTs to the active harmonic count `K` (`morph.cpp:440-478`).
9. Ramp every audio-rate continuous parameter across the 32-sample control period — never store `prev` and forget to use it (`wt_osc.h:222,712`; `lib_osc.cpp:975,991`).
10. Match mipmap taper guard thresholds and cosine start offsets identically (`wavetable_bank.cpp:300-301`: 80% vs 85% creates a passband ripple).
11. Select sample/granular mipmaps with `ceil(log2(effectiveStep))` using `|pitch * speed * rateMod|` — never `floor` or `round` on pitch alone (`sample_voice.cpp:156,917`; `granular.cpp:95,173`).
12. Guard loop crossfade indices against underflow (`pos - loopLen < 0` reads silence) and support reverse playback (`granular.cpp:485,739`).
13. Smooth filter cutoff in log-frequency with a snap-to-target epsilon so `tan()` memoization actually hits (`filter.cpp:141-143,176-177`).
14. Never share one scalar filter state across parallel delay lines (`combFeedbackLP_` in `filter.cpp:1294,1326`).
15. Scale every recursive filter coefficient and Poisson noise rate by `sampleRate` — never hardcode 44.1 kHz constants (`voice.cpp:41-84`, `classic_filters.h:940`).
16. Load atomic transport state *before* deriving `songSeconds` and advance it every 32-sample control tick inside a host block (`own_engine.cpp:1371-1378,1154`).
17. Index cross-modulation scratch buffers by `[t0..t0+n-1]` (or ring) so mid-period event splits stay block-size independent (`voice.cpp:1619-1623`).
18. Extend steal declick across all output buses, not just Main (`voice.cpp:302-304,1871-1883`), and skip `runFilter` passes completely when a filter is off (`voice.cpp:1751-1824`).
19. Verify both L and R assignments when copying single-oscillator tick structs (`subTick.panRight` unset in `voice.cpp:1390`) and never hardcode `QualityTier::high` on a low-tier voice (`voice.cpp:1433`).
20. Keep chaotic ODE integrators unconditionally accurate across the full rate range with RK2/RK4 + adaptive substepping instead of clamped forward Euler (`lfo.h:182-210`).
21. Size per-voice delay buffers to the active `sampleRate` and pool heavy per-type voice state (`FilterImpl` 14 delays, `LibOsc` 4 engines) to protect Cortex-A17's L2 cache (`docs/MPC.md:354`, `docs/QUALITY.md:127`).
22. Check constant-power unison center-voice gain across `spread = 0..1` (`sa + sb` inflates center gain by +3 dB at `spread = 0`, `wt_osc.h:384`).
23. Precompute invariant curve denominators (`1 / expm1(-power)`) and slew alphas at `compile()` time, not per control period (`mod_matrix.cpp:382,394-407`).
24. Replace UB `union` float/uint32 type-punning with `std::memcpy` (`fastmath.h:37,44`), and keep denormal hygiene consistent on non-FTZ desktop builds (`multisample.cpp:775`, `voice.cpp:1694`, `wt_osc.h:748`).
