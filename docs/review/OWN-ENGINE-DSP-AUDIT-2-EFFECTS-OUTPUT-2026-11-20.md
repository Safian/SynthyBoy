**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Scope:** Effects racks, instance pool, all 16 effect modules (`core/engine-own/fx/`, `core/fx-serum-a/`, `core/fx-serum-b/`), mixer bus routing and gain staging (`core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp`), master stage (`core/engine-own/dsp/master_stage.h`), shared output stage (`core/include/synthyboy/output_stage.h`), and audio hand-over to the MPC, JUCE, and CLAP plugin shells.

## Verdict

The own engine's effects and output architecture shows a stark split between genuinely reference-grade engineering at the very end of the chain and severe mathematical, acoustic, and ARMv7 CPU defects inside the effect modules themselves. The shared output stage (`OutputStage`), the $C^2$ master safety knee (`master_stage.h`), the participation-ratio voice-count gain ($G = n_{\text{eff}}^{-0.5}$), the coherent-sum bus makeup solver (`makeupOfBus`), the NEON 4-lane chorus kernel, and the Linkwitz–Riley 4th-order crossover in `Compressor` and `Utility` rest on sound mathematics and careful hardware awareness. By contrast, the core effects suffer from high-impact flaws that directly cost audio quality and real-time headroom: **Distortion** runs all 14 waveshapers at 1× host rate with zero oversampling and zero ADAA (and leaves X-Shaper / X-Shaper Asym as hard-clip stubs while recomputing `pow` and `exp` per sample); **Splitter L/M/H** puts a 4th-order all-pass (`AP4`) instead of a 2nd-order all-pass (`AP2`) on the low band, breaking flat crossover reconstruction, while **FxChain** omits inter-band latency alignment across all three splitters; **Delay HQ** executes 56 float divisions per sample per channel in `readLagrange8`, **Bode** executes 64 integer modulos (`% 129`) per sample in a Blackman Hilbert FIR whose quadrature response collapses below ~800 Hz (and hard-limits clean inputs at ±1.0 even with zero feedback), **Flanger** hard-clips its feedback loop at ±1.0 inside a ±1.8 headroom engine, **Reverb** allocates ~4.7 MB per instance (~14 MB across three racks) for all six algorithms simultaneously while calling 16 `sin` and 18 `exp` per sample in Vintage and producing negative high-frequency damping gain in Nitrous Space, **Convolve** uses flat 128-sample uniform partitioning up to 4,095 partitions with full complex FFTs and clicky un-crossfaded hot swaps, **Compressor** phase-cancels dry against wet in parallel multiband mix and still scans $O(W)$ per sample in Limit mode, **Hyper/Dimension** maintains 11 identical copies of the mono input and computes 23 `sin`/`cos` + 7 divisions per sample, and parameter smoothing is missing across almost every effect (plus sub-block-dependent in `fx-serum-a` and mid-period-blind in `voiceCountGain`), alongside real hand-over bugs in the CLAP and JUCE shells.

## Module-by-Module Deep Dive

### Module 1: Effect Chain, Racks, Pool, Bypass, Tail Detection, and Latency Compensation

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_chain.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_chain.cpp`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_module.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_latency.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_makeup.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_makeup.cpp`, `core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:329-380, 1498-1568, 1692-1736`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Rack & routing topology (`fx_chain.cpp:378-428, 708-729`, `own_engine.cpp:1692-1736`)**: Three `FxChain` instances own Main (`rackId = 0`), Bus 1 (`1`), and Bus 2 (`2`). Modules 0–12 (`kNumFxModules = 13`: Chorus through Convolve) and Splitters 13–15 (`SplitterLH`, `SplitterLMH`, `SplitterMS`) can sit in racks 0–2, and modules 0–12 can sit inside the 7 splitter band sub-racks (racks 3–4 L/H, 5–7 L/M/H, 8–9 M/S) owned by whichever chain holds that splitter (`fx_chain.cpp:412-419`). `fx.order.1..16` is repaired deterministically if duplicate choices exist (`fx_chain.cpp:382-394`), and each module at position `pos` receives sort key `64 * pos + 32` (`fxModuleSortKey`), interleaving cleanly with the 16 pool slots (`slotOrder[s]` in `0..1023`, instance 0 winning ties, `fx_chain.cpp:331-366`).
- **16-slot instance pool (`FxPool`, `fx_chain.h:43-87`, `fx_chain.cpp:67-163, 225-301`)**: Pool modules are instantiated and `prepare()`d off the audio thread (`OwnEngine::fxWorker` in realtime mode, `[code core/engine-own/own_engine.h:218-223]`), published via atomic `Slot::pending`, and retired via a 4-slot lock-free graveyard `Slot::grave[4]` collected on the next `typeChanged()`.
- **Crossfading & Bypass (`fx_chain.cpp:254-269, 452-464, 554-569, 658-705`)**: Pool slots perform a linear 10 ms crossfade (`sr * 0.01` samples) on on/off or type change (`runSlot`). Instance-0 modules only fade IN over **1 control period** (32 samples = 0.726 ms @ 44.1 kHz) when switched ON (`fadeIn[m]`), and have **no fade-out** when switched OFF. When a module is masked out by `Quality::fxMask` and `Quality::fxMakeup` is enabled, `FxChain` substitutes a smoothed loudness-compensation gain (`fx_makeup.h/.cpp`) fed by a 5-band K-weighted `ProgramMeter` (`fx_chain.cpp:429-450, 643-655`); for Bus 1/2, makeup is deferred to `OwnEngine::makeupOfBus()` (`own_engine.cpp:329-380`), which measures Main/Bus powers and cross-correlation to solve the coherent-sum quadratic $P_m + G^2 P_b + 2GC = P_m + P_b P_{\text{mod}} + 2AC$.
- **Silence / tail detection (`fx_chain.cpp:505-512, 757-781`, `fx_module.h:150-188`)**: On tier `low`, (1) `FxChain::process` tracks `quietRun` whenever `peakIn == 0.0f && peakOut < 1e-6f` (-120 dBFS); once `quietRun >= holdSamples` (20 s if Delay or any pool slot is running, 1 s otherwise), it skips the rack and calls `idleRack()` to keep free-running LFO phases advancing; (2) `FxModule::process` (`muteSkip`) skips zero-latency modules whose `mix == 0` for two consecutive periods after flushing any lingering tail into a scratch buffer until `< -120 dBFS` for `flushSeconds() * 44100.0f` samples.
- **Latency compensation (`fx_latency.h:16-101`, `own_engine.cpp:1549-1567`)**: `LatencyPlan::compute` aligns voices-to-Main, Bus 1/2 inputs and outputs, Main output, and Direct via `IntDelay` integer circular buffers (`kMax = 255` samples).

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Parallel path phase alignment**: Summing two parallel copies of a signal with a relative integer sample delay $\Delta$ creates a linear comb filter $|1 + e^{-j\omega \Delta}| = 2|\cos(\omega \Delta / 2)|$, carving periodic complete nulls every $f_s / \Delta$ Hz (e.g., a 64-sample Bode mismatch at 44.1 kHz puts the first null at 345 Hz and 32 notches across the spectrum). Exact sample alignment across Main, Bus 1/2, Direct, and splitter bands is therefore mandatory whenever any parallel branch contains a non-zero-latency module.
- **Crossfade duration and clicks**: A step discontinuity of amplitude $A$ injects broadband impulse energy ($\propto A^2$) audible as a sharp click; a 0.73 ms linear ramp (instance 0 fade-in) has a corner around ~1.4 kHz (still mildly audible as a "tick" on low bass tones), whereas a 10 ms raised-cosine or linear crossfade pushes transition energy below ~100 Hz where temporal masking hides it. An instantaneous drop (instance 0 bypass/off, `fx.order` reorder, `IntDelay::clear()`) produces a full-amplitude click and unnatural tail truncation.
- **Coherent vs incoherent bus makeup**: Solving $P_m + G^2 P_b + 2GC = P_m + P_b P_{\text{mod}} + 2AC$ (`own_engine.cpp:358-364`) is acoustically well-founded: when a wet reverb on a parallel send is bypassed, the dry send is coherent ($C > 0$) with Main, whereas the active reverb was uncorrelated ($A \approx 0$), so naive power-gain matching would overestimate loudness by up to +3 to +6 dB.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Lock-free module creation + graveyard retirement (`FxPool`) and exact graph latency planning (`LatencyPlan`) follow standard real-time audio graph engineering (e.g., Zölzer, *DAFX*, 2011; Ross Bencina, *"Real-time audio programming 101"*, 2011). The coherent-sum `makeupOfBus` model is an unusually thoughtful original contribution.
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Missing splitter inter-band latency alignment** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_chain.cpp:583-610]`): `docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:360` explicitly specifies that every splitter band waits `slowest - own`, and `FxChain::rackLatency()` computes `max` of band latencies (`fx_chain.cpp:539-544`), yet `runModule()` merges the bands directly with zero delay lines.
  2. **Asymmetric fade rules**: Pool slots get a 10 ms crossfade on both ON and OFF, while instance-0 modules get a 0.73 ms fade-in and zero fade-out (`fx_chain.cpp:456-464, 554-569`), and `fx.order` changes have no crossfade.
  3. **Hardcoded 44.1 kHz flush counter** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_module.h:158]`): `quietNeeded = static_cast<int>(flushSeconds() * 44100.0f)` ignores the actual `sampleRate` (cutting hold time by >2× at 96 kHz).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Splitter comb-filtering** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_chain.cpp:583-610]`): Placing Bode (64 smp) or Limiter (66–144 smp) in one band of L/H, L/M/H, or M/S leaves the sibling bands unaligned on `merge()`.
- **Clicks on bypass, reorder, and latency change**:
  - Turning any instance-0 effect OFF (`fx_chain.cpp:399, 454`) drops it instantaneously (hard click + tail cut-off).
  - Changing any latency module triggers `IntDelay::set()` -> `clear()` (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_latency.h:26]`), zeroing up to 255 samples of live audio on every aligned bus.
  - `IntDelay::kMax = 255` (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_latency.h:19]`) silently clamps chains exceeding 255 samples (e.g., 2×Bode + Limiter at 96 kHz = 128 + 144 = 272 samples, or 4×Bode = 256 samples).
- **Incomplete `muteSkip` coverage** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:186, 584, 639, 691, 779]`): `Compressor`, `HyperDimension`, `Utility`, `Bode`, and `Convolve` never call `wetMix()`, so `muteSkip` never fires for them at `mix = 0`.
- **Event-split dependence in `makeupOfBus`** (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:349, 376, 1710]`): Runs per sub-block `n` and ramps `mk` over `n` samples instead of the 32-sample control period.
- **Potential silent leak in `FxPool::retire`** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_chain.cpp:117-124]`): Rapid type automation (>4 changes before message thread collects) permanently leaks retired `FxModule` instances (each Reverb is ~4.7 MB!).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-block / per-sample**: `FxChain::update` runs `1.4 to 1.8 %` of the Key 61 block `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:361]`, partly because `inputsSame` is `false` whenever any global modulation is active (`gnumTouched > 0`, `own_engine.cpp:1473`) and because splitters call `dbToGain` even when off (`fx_chain.cpp:422-426`). `finiteBlock` (`fx_chain.cpp:51-57`) scans every active module's output in scalar C++ (`!std::isfinite`), whereas `peak::peakAbsPair` uses NEON (`peak.h:88-109`).
- **Tail hold waste (`fx_chain.cpp:508-511`)**: Any running pool slot (even Utility or EQ) forces `holdSamples = 20.0 * sr`, keeping the entire rack awake for 20 seconds of silence on tier `low`.
- **Muted splitter band waste (`fx_chain.cpp:587-608`)**: When `low_bypass` / `mid_bypass` / `high_bypass` / `side_bypass` is ON, `merge()` multiplies the band by `0.0f`, yet `runRack()` still processes every effect in that band!
- **Memory**: `FxChain` pre-allocates all 13 `inst[m]` modules for all 3 racks (Main, Bus 1, Bus 2) in `prepare()` (`own_engine.cpp:452-458`), costing **~26 MB** across the 3 racks (dominated by 3×Reverb ~14.1 MB, 3×Delay ~6.0 MB, 3×Bode ~2.6 MB, 3×Flanger ~1.5 MB, 3×FX Filter ~1.1 MB, 3×Chorus ~0.8 MB) and **73.6 ms** in `prepare_fx` `[measured: docs/MPC.md:327]`.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Add inter-band `IntDelay` alignment for all three splitters** in `FxChain::runModule` (`fx_chain.cpp:583-610`). *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Sine-sweep + impulse alignment test with Bode in Splitter L/H Low band.
2. **Add 10 ms crossfade for instance-0 ON/OFF transitions** (matching `FxPool::runSlot`), raise `IntDelay::kMax` to 1023 with short crossfade on delay change, and fix `flushSeconds() * sampleRate` in `fx_module.h:158`. *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Click test toggling Reverb/Delay OFF mid-note; 96 kHz flush timing test.
3. **Skip `runRack` for muted splitter bands, inspect pool slot type for 20 s hold, and wire `wetMix()` in Compressor (when `latency == 0`), Hyper, Utility, Convolve**. *Gain:* Small–Medium (CPU on Key 61). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low` on presets with muted splitter bands or `mix == 0` effects.

### Module 2: Chorus, Flanger, and Phaser

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:157-665`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_dsp.h:17-63, 140-144`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:124-158, 411-487`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Chorus (`fx_effects.h:157-508`)**: `V = 1..4` voice pairs × 2 delay groups (`delay1`, `delay2`) × stereo = up to 16 parallel feedback delay lines (all stored contiguously in `store`, sharing `linePos`), read with 4-point Catmull-Rom cubic interpolation, saturated in the feedback loop via `loopLimit` (`±8.0`), band-limited by `LoopBand` (two one-pole low-passes subtracted, gain-compensated by `f_lo/f_hi + 1`), modulated by quadrature sine LFOs (`c` offset +0.25, `vi` offset +0.5, pair offset `0.25*i/V`), summed with fixed `0.53f`, and blended via equal-power `MixGains`. Has an ARM NEON 4-lane kernel (`tapGroupNeon` + `refreshFour` using `neon_cmp.h`, `fx_effects.h:280-465`).
- **Flanger (`fx_effects.h:511-567`)**: Stereo feedback delay line (Catmull-Rom cubic) modulated by a stereo-offset triangle LFO around `1 / centerHz` (clamped `>= 0.05 ms`), feedback hard-clamped to `[-1.0, +1.0]` with no loop filter, mixed via half-range equal-power (`m = 0.5 * mix`, so wet reaches at most -3 dB).
- **Phaser (`fx_effects.h:569-665`)**: 12 cascaded 1st-order ZDF/TPT all-pass stages per channel (`lp = (x - z)*a + z; z = 2*lp - z; out = x - 2*lp`), tapping after stages 4, 8, and 12 blended via `blend`. Cutoff modulated in semitones by a stereo-offset triangle LFO (`±depthSemis`), optionally spread across the 12 stages by `depth2` (`[-depth2/2, +depth2/2]`). Feedback passes through a 1-sample-delayed two-one-pole bandpass (`[hz/20, hz*20]`) and `tanh(fbk * bp)`. Wet is `0.5 * (dry + ap)` (producing notches at `0.4142 fc, 2.4142 fc` for 4 stages), blended linearly with `dry`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Delay-line interpolation**: Catmull-Rom cubic (`readCubic`) is a $C^1$ piecewise-cubic FIR interpolator with $-3$ dB roll-off around $0.35\text{–}0.40\,f_s$ and stopband image attenuation of ~35–45 dB at mid-high frequencies (sufficient for chorus/flanger modulation; the measured 1.5 dB worst harmonic difference between 44.1 and 48 kHz in Chorus at 40% feedback comes from cumulative Catmull-Rom HF damping inside the feedback loop `[measured: docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:334]`).
- **Step-and-hold delay modulation zipper**: On tier `low`, Chorus refreshes `period[]` only every 4 samples (`fx_effects.h:229`). For a sinusoidal delay $d(t) = d_0 + A\sin(2\pi f_{\text{lfo}} t)$, maximum delay slew is $2\pi f_{\text{lfo}} A$ samples/sample. At `depth = 1.0` ($A = 15\text{ ms} = 661.5$ samples @ 44.1 kHz) and $f_{\text{lfo}} = 4\text{ Hz}$, the delay jumps by $4 \times 2\pi(4)(0.015) \approx 1.51$ samples every 4 samples! That introduces sample-and-hold modulation images at $f_{\text{sig}} \pm k \cdot 11025\text{ Hz}$ (and even at 1 Hz depth 1.0, a 0.38-sample jump every 4 samples creates audible high-frequency grit). Similarly, Phaser's 8-sample coefficient hold on tier `low` (`fx_effects.h:608`) modulates notch frequencies in 5.51 kHz steps.
- **All-pass phasing & feedback stability**: Each TPT 1st-order all-pass has unity magnitude $|H(e^{j\omega})| = 1$ and phase shifting from $0$ to $-\pi$ through $-\pi/2$ at $f_c$. Summing `0.5 * (dry + ap)` places $N/2$ deep spectral notches where total phase hits odd multiples of $\pi$. Because `z` is TPT/trapezoidal, the individual stages are unconditionally stable for all $f_c < f_s/2$, and `tanh` bounds the 1-sample-delayed outer feedback loop cleanly for `fbk <= 1.0`.
- **Stereo & loudness laws**: Chorus's 90° quadrature L/R phase offset and 180° A/B offset decorrelate L and R well for stereo width (with acceptable mono sum when `depth > 0`), **but** fixed output factor `0.53f` regardless of `V = 1..4` causes uncorrelated wet power to scale as $V$ (+6 dB from 1 to 4 voice pairs, +12 dB coherent). Flanger's half-range equal-power mix (`cos(pi/4 * mix)`, `sin(pi/4 * mix)`) guarantees deep through-zero-like comb notches at `mix = 100%` because dry and wet reach equal amplitude (`0.7071`) at max mix.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Catmull-Rom modulated delay lines with quadrature LFOs (Dattorro, *"Effect Design, Part 2: Delay-Line Modulation and Chorus"*, JAES 1997) and TPT 1st-order all-pass phasers (Zavalishin, *The Art of VA Filter Design*, 2012; Julius O. Smith, *Physical Audio Signal Processing*, 2010) are solid modern practice. Chorus's `tapGroupNeon` + `neon_cmp.h` bit-pattern denormal flushing is well-engineered for ARMv7 (`0.05 ms` saved per block in Round 12 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:336]`, `0.201 ms/block` `[measured: docs/MPC.md:97]`).
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Flanger hard-clipping at ±1.0 (`fx_effects.h:556`)** violates the engine's `±1.8` headroom and lacks DC blocking.
  2. **Step-and-hold LFO refresh without linear interpolation** in tier `low` Chorus (every 4 samples) and Phaser (every 8 samples): linearly interpolating `period` across the 4 samples (or `aS` across 8 samples) costs only 1 add per sample and eliminates 11 kHz / 5.5 kHz zipper sidebands completely.
  3. **No transport phase sync**: `06-effects.md:38, 181` specify locking synced Chorus/Phaser LFO phase to host song position, yet `fx_modules.cpp:140, 427, 472` only compute rate and free-run.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Flanger headroom clipping & DC latch** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:537, 556]`): Any input $> 1.0$ (or `in + f*rd > 1.0`) is hard-clipped at `±1.0` at 1× sample rate (aliasing!), and at `feedback = +1.0` DC builds up indefinitely to ±1.0 with no loop HPF/DC blocker.
- **Tier `low` Chorus/Phaser step-hold zipper** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:229, 608]`): Staircase delay/cutoff jumps at 11.025 kHz (Chorus) and 5.51 kHz (Phaser).
- **Chorus voice-count loudness jump** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:270-271]`): Constant `0.53f` yields +6 dB RMS shift between `voices = 1` and `voices = 4`.
- **Phaser denormals** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:651-652]`): `bpA[c]` and `bpB[c]` lack `flushDenormal()`.
- **Unsmoothed parameters**: `mix`, `feedback`, `depth`, `delay1/2`, `centerHz`, `blend`, `cutoff` step every 32 samples across all three effects (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:198, 539, 594, 655]`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Chorus**: Well-vectorized on ARM NEON (`tapGroupNeon` + `refreshFour`), `0.201 ms/block` on Key 61 `[measured: docs/MPC.md:97]`. Memory: 16 lines × 4096 floats = **256 KB/instance** (768 KB across 3 racks), exceeding L1D (32 KB) when all 16 taps are active.
- **Flanger**: Scalar loop; **per-sample `p.rate / sr` division** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:544]`). Memory: `prepare(sr * 0.9)` rounds to 65,536 floats × 2 = **512 KB/instance** (1.5 MB across 3 racks), **8× larger** than the actual max delay (100 ms at `centerHz = 20 Hz, depth = 1` -> 8192 floats = 64 KB/instance). Masked on Key 61 `low`.
- **Phaser**: Scalar loop (`3.5 %` of Key 61 block on `Mid Side Halo` `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:353]`). **Per-sample `p.rate / sr` division** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:602]`). On medium/high (`fast == false`), runs `exp2 + 2*exp + tan + div` per sample per channel, exploding to **12 `exp2` + 12 `tan` + 12 divisions per sample per channel** when `depth2 != 0` (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:614-625]`)! On ARM, Phaser's L/R channels are trivially 2-lane vectorizable (or 4-stage parallel with fixed cutoff).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Linearly interpolate Chorus `period` across its 4-sample step and Phaser `aS` across its 8-sample step**, and compute Phaser `depth2` stage ratios as precomputed block constants (or sub-block interpolated) instead of 12 `exp2`/`tan`/divs per sample. *Gain:* Medium–Large (quality on Key 61 + CPU on XL). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 1 kHz sine FFT under 5 Hz Chorus/Phaser LFO checking elimination of 11.025 / 5.51 kHz spurs; XL `tier_bench` with `phaser.depth2 = 24`.
2. **Fix Flanger feedback clamp (`±8.0` or soft knee above `±1.8`), add 10 Hz DC blocker, shrink buffer from `0.9*sr` to `0.12*sr`, and hoist `p.rate / sr` in Flanger and Phaser** + add `flushDenormal` to Phaser `bpA`/`bpB`. *Gain:* Medium (quality + 1.3 MB RAM + CPU). *Risk:* Low–Medium (flanger saturation above 0 dBFS changes). *Proving measurement:* 1.5-peak sine THD test through Flanger; DC stability test at `feedback = +100%`.
3. **Ramp `mix` / `feedback` across `kControlPeriod` and lock synced LFO phases to `f.songBeats`**. *Gain:* Small–Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Zipper FFT on LFO-modulated mix; transport-locate phase null test.

### Module 3: Delay

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:55-155`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_dsp.h:17-86`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:216-261`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- Stereo circular delay lines (`DelayLine`, power-of-two capacity `>= 4.2 * sr`), supporting 5 styles (`0` Mono, `1` Stereo, `2` Ping pong, `3` Mid ping pong, `4` Tap->Delay), free time or tempo-synced divisions (`4 bars .. 1/64`, dotted/triplet), independent `offsetL`/`offsetR` multipliers, 20 ms half-life tape-glide smoothing on reciprocal time `f = 1/T`, `LoopBand` feedback bandpass (`[cutoff - spread*96st, cutoff + spread*96st]`), `loopLimit` (`±8.0`), Catmull-Rom cubic (`hq == false`) or 8-point Lagrange (`hq == true`) interpolation, and equal-power `MixGains`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Tape glide (`fx_effects.h:89-104`)**: Smoothing `fLast = 1 / T` exponentially with a 20 ms half-life causes delay length $T(t) = 1 / f(t)$ to change smoothly, creating a Doppler pitch shift $f_{\text{out}}/f_{\text{in}} = 1 - dT/dt$ mimicking analog tape speed inertia without abrupt comb clicks. Because `f` is smoothed rather than `T`, shortening delay bends pitch slightly faster at the onset than lengthening.
- **Interpolation accuracy (`readCubic` vs `readLagrange8`)**: 4-point Catmull-Rom attenuates highs gently during non-integer delays and glides; 8-point (7th-order) Lagrange maximally-flat fractional delay (`fx_dsp.h:65-80`) pushes the -3 dB passband out to ~0.43 $f_s$ (preserving shimmer on bright repeats across many feedback passes), at the cost of ringing on discontinuous impulses if overshoot occurs (Laakso et al., 1996).
- **Ping-pong feedback law (`fx_effects.h:124-135`)**: Right lane feeds back at `1.0`, left lane at `fb`, with cross-channel writes (`line[0]` gets `wr`, `line[1]` gets `wl`), matching `06-effects.md:51`. Each full L–R cycle attenuates by `fb` (so each ping-pong pair stays balanced in loudness across the stereo field, though right-only input takes one unattenuated hop before seeing `fb`).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Maximal-flatness Lagrange interpolation and Farrow/barycentric evaluation are classic fractional-delay theory (Laakso, Välimäki, Karjalainen & Laine, *"Splitting the Unit Delay"*, IEEE Signal Processing Magazine, 1996).
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Catastrophic naive `readLagrange8` implementation (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_dsp.h:65-80]`)**: Evaluates the raw textbook product $\prod_{m \ne k} (t - m)/(k - m)$ inside a doubly nested 8×8 loop, executing **56 scalar float divisions per sample per channel = 112 float divisions per stereo sample!** Using the second barycentric Lagrange form (or precomputed constant reciprocals `1/(k-m)` / `(-1)^k / (k!(7-k)!)`, Berrut & Trefethen 2004) reduces 56 divisions to **0 divisions** (just 16–24 multiplies + 1 precomputed factor, or 8-tap Farrow structure).
  2. **Per-sample divisions in steady state (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:104]`)**: Computes `sr / fLast[0]` and `sr / fLast[1]` every single sample even when `fLast == fTarget` (99.9% of steady playback), and recomputes `std::pow(0.5f, 1/(0.02*sr))` every 32 samples (`line 96`) instead of caching in `prepare()`.
  3. **Memory**: Allocates `next_pow2(4.2 * sr)` = 262,144 floats × 2 = **2.0 MB per Delay instance = 6.0 MB across 3 racks** unconditionally, even when Delay is OFF (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:68]`).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Unsmoothed `mix`, `feedback`, `cutoff`, `spread`** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:81, 97-99]`): Modulating `delay.mix` or `delay.feedback` steps every 32 samples (1378 Hz zipper noise).
- **Offset clamp mismatch**: `fx_chain.cpp:506` and `fx_modules.cpp:226` assume echoes up to `16 s` (`4 s * offset`), whereas `Delay::process` clamps `tL` and `tR` to `4.0f` s (`fx_effects.h:89, 104`), silently capping `offset > 1` on long delays.
- **No denormal flush in `DelayLine::push`** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_dsp.h:30]`): Safe in Delay only because `LoopBand` flushes denormals in `hi`/`lo` before `push()`.
- **Dead state**: Unused member `fbRead[2]` (`fx_effects.h:75, 153`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Non-HQ mode**: Cheap (0.1–0.2% of Key 61 block saveable by caching `glide`, `LoopBand`, and skipping `sr / fLast` when steady `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:398]`).
- **HQ mode (`delay.hq = 1`)**: `112` scalar float divisions per stereo sample (`~14,336` float divisions per 128-frame block; on Cortex-A17 VFP `vdiv.f32` has ~14-cycle latency, costing ~100–200k cycles = **~5–10% of the entire Key 61 audio budget for Delay HQ alone** `[hypothesis: tier_bench --config low --sections with delay.hq=1 vs delay.hq=0 on Key 61 will measure >5% block time jump from readLagrange8]`).
- **Cache footprint**: At 2.0 MB per Delay instance, long delays (>100 ms) stream continuously out of Cortex-A17 L2 cache (unavoidable for long delays, though lazy-allocating on `on` or sizing to host rate avoids wasting 4 MB on unused Bus 1/2 delays).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Rewrite `DelayLine::readLagrange8` using precomputed denominator constants `kInvDen[8]` (`-1/5040, +1/720, -1/240, +1/144, ...`) and the product trick `(t+3)(t+2)...(t-4)` (or Horner/Farrow)** — eliminating all 56 divisions/channel/sample. *Gain:* Large (CPU when `delay.hq = 1`). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Microbenchmark of `readLagrange8` before vs after + float32 max-diff check (< 1e-6).
2. **Cache `glide` in `prepare()`, cache `LoopBand` coefficients when `(cutoff, spread)` are unchanged, and skip `sr / fLast` when `|fLast - fTarget| < 1e-7f`**. *Gain:* Small (CPU, ~0.1–0.2% on Key 61 `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:398]`). *Risk:* Low (bit-identical). *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low --sections` on delay presets.
3. **Ramp `mix` and `feedback` across `kControlPeriod`**. *Gain:* Small–Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 1 kHz sine FFT under 5 Hz LFO on `delay.mix`.

### Module 4: Reverb (All Six Algorithms)

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_reverb.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:490-551`.

#### (a) Algorithm
All 6 types share an input polarity-inverted 1-pole bandpass (`inLp[c+2] - inLp[c]`, `fx_reverb.h:183-190`), post-tank M/S `width`, post-tank stereo `pre[2]` pre-delay (`0..300 ms`), and equal-power `MixGains`:
- **Type 0 (Vital 16-line FDN, `fx_reverb.h:214-275`)**: Stereo input (L -> lines 0..7, R -> lines 8..15), 16 modulated delay lines (`readLagrange4`, quadrature `cos/sin` LFOs) in series with 16 Schroeder all-passes (`kA[k]*scale`, `c = 0.6`), mixed by the 16×16 orthogonal Kronecker Householder matrix $H_4 \otimes H_4$ (`y = v + 0.25 S - 0.5 C - 0.5 R`), damped per line by 1-pole high/low shelves, tapped *before* `fdnGain[k]` so wet onset level is independent of `decay`/`size`.
- **Type 1 (Plate — Dattorro 1997, `fx_reverb.h:435-471`)**: Mono input -> 4 input Schroeder all-passes -> figure-8 two-branch cross-coupled tank (modulated all-pass, `del1`, `DampStage * g`, fixed `ap2`, `del2`), 7 stereo output taps per side + 6-tap stereo early reflections (`erLine`).
- **Types 2, 4, 5 (Hall, Nitrous, Basin — 8-line FDN, `fx_reverb.h:330-433`)**: Mono input -> 4 series input all-passes -> 8 modulated delay lines (`net[8]`, glide 0.002/sample on `size`) -> 8×8 orthonormal Walsh–Hadamard matrix -> 8 loop all-passes (`netAp[8]`) -> 8 `DampStage` (TPT high/low shelves) -> `netGain[k]` + 6-tap ER. Nitrous derives line lengths from physical 3D room dimensions `(a, b, c) / 343 m/s`.
- **Type 3 (Vintage — Freeverb-style, `fx_reverb.h:473-502`)**: Per channel (R offset +23 samples @ 44.1 kHz): 1 input all-pass -> 8 parallel modulated feedback combs (`vComb[2][8]`) with `DampStage` -> 4 series all-passes (`vAp[2][4]`) -> 7 kHz one-pole low-pass + 6-tap ER.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Modal density & metallic ringing**: Schroeder's criterion requires roughly $\ge 0.15\text{–}0.25$ modes/Hz (total delay length $\sum L_k \ge 0.15\text{–}0.25 \cdot RT_{60}$ seconds) with mutually prime/incommensurate lengths and a dense unitary feedback matrix to avoid isolated metallic resonances ("ringing"). Type 0 (16 lines + 16 all-passes, total delay ~2.5 s at `size = 50%` @ 44.1 kHz) and Types 2/4/5 (8-line Hadamard FDN + 8 loop all-passes + 4 input diffusers) provide high modal and echo density; Type 1 (Dattorro figure-8 plate) achieves silky density through 4 input diffusers + nested tank all-passes and LFO modulation. Type 3 (Vintage, 8 parallel uncoupled combs) has much lower echo density and inherently exhibits classic Freeverb metallic flutter/ringing on impulses when `chorusAmount == 0`.
- **Decay accuracy (RT60)**: For a lossless orthogonal feedback matrix, uniform energy decay $g_k = 10^{-3 L_{\text{loop},k} / T} = e^{-\ln(1000) L_{\text{loop},k} / T}$ guarantees exact frequency-independent $RT_{60} = T$ (Jot 1991). Types 1–5 include all-pass delays in the round-trip time and hit $RT_{60} \approx T$ accurately (e.g., 2.01 / 1.94 / 1.99 / 2.01 s for 2000 ms `[measured: docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:311-315]`). Type 0 omits `kA[k]` from `fdnGain[k]` (`fx_reverb.h:224`), inflating actual $RT_{60}$ up to **3.6×** at small sizes (500 ms -> 1.81 s at `size = 20%` `[measured: docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:228]`) and causing an **18% RT60 shift between 44.1 and 48 kHz** `[measured: docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:334]` because `apLine` scales by power-of-two `scale` (`1` at 44.1k, `2` at 48k -> doubled all-pass length at 48 kHz!).
- **Negative HF damping gain bug in Nitrous Space (`fx_reverb.h:295, 300`)**: `ah = 1 - (1 - ahRaw) * kRoomK[room]`. For `room == 0` (Space), `kRoomK[0] = 1.45`. Whenever `highShelfDb < -9.9 dB` (`ahRaw < 0.3103`), `ah` becomes **negative** (reaching `-0.3585` at `-24 dB`), causing the high shelf `lp + ah*(x - lp)` to invert the polarity of high frequencies on every pass and *increase* HF tail decay energy relative to `ah = 0`!

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Unitary FDNs with per-line absorptive damping filters (Jot & Chaigne, *"Digital Delay Networks for Designing Artificial Reverberators"*, AES 1991; Schlecht & Habets, *"On Lossless Feedback Delay Networks"*, IEEE TSP 2017) and the Dattorro plate (Dattorro, *"Effect Design, Part 1: Reverberator and Other Filters"*, JAES 1997) are industry-standard designs.
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **O(N²) Walsh–Hadamard matrix multiply (`fx_reverb.h:415-421`)**: Computes an 8×8 matrix product with 64 iterations and bit-popcount sign branching per sample instead of a 3-stage butterfly Fast Walsh–Hadamard Transform (24 add/subs, zero branches, trivially NEON-vectorizable).
  2. **Massive redundant per-sample `libm` & cubic reads**: Calling `gainFor()` (`exp` + div) 16×/sample in Vintage and 1×/sample in Plate, `OnePole::coefficient(7000)` (`exp` + div) 2×/sample in Vintage, 16 `sin`/`cos` per sample in FDN16 (only 4 unique phases exist!), and `readCubic` on **exact integer constant** all-pass and plate output taps.
  3. **6× memory bloat**: Allocating buffers for all 6 mutually exclusive reverb topologies simultaneously (~4.7 MB/instance).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Nitrous Space HF polarity inversion (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_reverb.h:295, 300]`)**: `ah` goes negative for `highShelfDb < -9.9 dB` (`-0.36` at `-24 dB`).
- **Pre-delay zipper (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_reverb.h:169, 179, 205]`)**: `pdCur` steps once per 32 samples (1378 Hz pitch-zipper on pre-delay modulation), and being post-tank bends the entire wet reverb tail including early reflections.
- **Type 0 `size` clicks (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_reverb.h:223]`)**: `fdnLen[k]` jumps instantaneously every 32 samples without gliding (unlike `netLen[k]` in Types 2/4/5 which glides by `0.002`/sample at `line 403`), causing clicks on `reverb.size` automation.
- **Rate-dependent Type 0 RT60 (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_reverb.h:79-81, 246]`)**: `scale` jumps from `1` at 44.1 kHz to `2` at 48 kHz, doubling all-pass lengths and shifting RT60 by 18% `[measured: docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:334]`.
- **Unsmoothed `mix` and `width`** (`fx_reverb.h:163, 201`) step every 32 samples.
- **Algorithm switch click**: Switching `reverb.type` while sounding immediately jumps to a different tank with zero crossfade and without resetting the newly selected tank's stale buffers!

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-sample cost per algorithm** (scalar, **zero NEON** anywhere in `fx_reverb.h`):
  - *Type 0 (FDN16)*: 16 `sin`/`cos` + 16 `readLagrange4` + 16 `readCubic` (all-passes) + 1 div + 1 `floor` per sample.
  - *Type 1 (Plate)*: 1 `exp` + 1 div (`gainFor`) + 2 `sin` + **21 `readCubic`** (tank + taps) + 12 ER `readCubic` = **33 cubic reads/sample**!
  - *Types 2/4/5 (8-FDN)*: 8 `sin` + 8 `readCubic` (lines) + 12 `readCubic` (all-passes) + 12 ER `readCubic` + 64-iter nested Hadamard loop = **32 cubic reads + 8 `sin` + 64 popcount branches/sample**!
  - *Type 3 (Vintage)*: **16 `sin` + 18 `exp` + 18 divs** + 26 `readCubic` + 12 ER `readCubic` = **38 cubic reads + 34 transcendentals + 18 divs/sample**! (`[hypothesis: tier_bench with fx=all on Key 61 or XL running Reverb Type 3 Vintage will be the slowest reverb algorithm by >2x solely due to the 18 per-sample exp() calls]`).
- **Memory & cache**: Each `Reverb` allocates ~2.13 MB (FDN16) + ~1.05 MB (8-FDN) + ~0.78 MB (Plate) + ~0.58 MB (Vintage) + ~0.16 MB (ER+Pre) = **~4.7 MB per instance** (**~14.1 MB across Main/Bus 1/Bus 2**!), far exceeding L2 cache and contributing heavily to `prepare_fx = 73.6 ms` `[measured: docs/MPC.md:327]`.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Hoist all block-constant `exp`/divs (`gainFor`, `OnePole::coefficient(7000)`), replace 8×8 Hadamard loop with 3-stage FWHT, compute only 4 unique trig phases in FDN16 (0 when `depth == 0`), skip ER taps when `erLevel == 0`, and replace `readCubic` on integer constant delays (`apLine`, `inAp`, `netAp`, `plDel`, `plAp2`, `vInAp`, `vAp`, `erLine`) with direct ring reads** (or linear interpolation for fractional fixed taps). *Gain:* Large (CPU: 2×–5× across all 6 algorithms, potentially making Reverb viable even on Key 61!). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* XL and Key 61 (`fx=all`) `tier_bench` across all 6 `reverb.type` values + RT60/R3 vector verification.
2. **Share one pooled delay arena across the 6 reverb types** (sizing to max single algorithm ~2.2 MB instead of sum ~4.7 MB, or lazy-allocating off-RT for Bus 1/2). *Gain:* Large (RAM: saves ~7.5–12 MB; startup `prepare_fx`). *Risk:* Low (requires `reset()` on `type` change, which also fixes stale-tail bleed!). *Proving measurement:* `VmRSS` and `prepare_fx` ms before vs after.
3. **Clamp Nitrous `ah >= 0.0f`, ramp `pdCur` per sample, smooth Type 0 `fdnLen` on size changes, and vectorize FDN16 / 8-FDN 4-wide in NEON**. *Gain:* Medium–Large (quality + ARM CPU). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Nitrous Space impulse at `highShelfDb = -24 dB`; predelay-sweep zipper FFT; NEON vs scalar diff check.

### Module 5: Compressor (Multiband, Upward/Downward, Limit, Serum)

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:161-213`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Crossover (`fx_compressor.h:57-77, 119-145`)**: 4th-order Linkwitz–Riley (LR4 = two cascaded 2nd-order Butterworth DF2T biquads, $Q = 0.7071$) supporting Single band (`bands == 3`), 2-band Low/High (`bands == 1, 2`), and 3-band Multiband (`bands == 0`). In 3-band mode, the Low band passes through `LR4_LP(f2) + LR4_HP(f2)` (`ap[c][0..3]`, 4 biquads) to match the all-pass phase shift of the Mid+High upper split.
- **Detectors & Modes (`fx_compressor.h:146-216`)**:
  - *Mode 0 (Compress)*: Stereo-linked power detector $x^2 = \frac{1}{2}(L^2 + R^2)$ with asymmetric 1-pole attack/release `env = (x^2 + env * N) / (N + 1)`, band-scaled base times (2.8/1.4/0.7 ms attack, 40/28/15 ms release), downward gain `(thrU / envHi)^(upperRatio/2)` and upward/expander gain `(thrL / envLo)^(lowerRatio/2)` clamped to `[0, 32]`, followed by per-band makeup gain.
  - *Mode 1 (Limit)*: Replaces the downward stage with a per-band 1.5 ms look-ahead brickwall limiter (`lookAhead = round(0.0015 * sr)` = 66 @ 44.1k, 72 @ 48k, 144 @ 96k), using 3-point quadratic inter-sample peak estimation (`m = -0.125*prev2 + 0.75*prev + 0.375*in`), a running minimum over `la + 1` samples, 1-pole release, and a length-`(la + 1)` boxcar smoother; dry path is delayed by `lookAhead` via `dry[2]`.
  - *Mode 2 (Serum)*: Stereo peak detector with 1 ms peak hold decay, hard knee in dB (`20 * log10(pk)`), smoothed gain reduction with log-scaled attack/release (`0.1 .. 1000 ms`), converted back via `10^(-gr/20)`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **LR4 reconstruction & parallel-mix cancellation**: A 4th-order Linkwitz–Riley pair satisfies $H_{\text{LP}4}(s) + H_{\text{HP}4}(s) = \frac{\omega_c^4 + s^4}{(s^2 + \sqrt{2}\omega_c s + \omega_c^2)^2} = \frac{s^2 - \sqrt{2}\omega_c s + \omega_c^2}{s^2 + \sqrt{2}\omega_c s + \omega_c^2} = A_2(s)$ — unity magnitude everywhere with a 2nd-order all-pass phase shift reaching $-180^\circ$ at $f_c$ (Linkwitz 1976). Therefore, summing the 3 crossover bands (with Low phase-aligned through $A_2(f_2)$) reconstructs unity magnitude! **However**, in `fx_compressor.h:207-215`, parallel wet/dry blending (`d + (out - d) * mix`) mixes the phase-shifted wet sum $A_2(f_1)A_2(f_2)\,X$ against the **unfiltered dry input** $X$. At any frequency where $\angle(A_2(f_1)A_2(f_2)) = \pm 180^\circ$ (near $f_1$ and $f_2$), $X + A_2 X = 0$ — producing **deep comb notches (complete cancellation at `mix = 50%`)** even with zero compression!
- **Power-domain vs dB-domain ballistics**: Mode 0 filters instantaneous power $x^2$ (true RMS² ballistics, avoiding log/exp inside the detector loop), whereas Mode 2 (Serum) tracks peak with 1 ms hold and smooths gain reduction in dB (Giannoulis et al., 2012 "smooth decoupled peak detector" — musically transparent on transients with minimal harmonic ripple).
- **Look-ahead limiter proof**: In `Limiter::process`, taking the minimum of required gain over `[t - la, t]` and convolving with a boxcar of length `la + 1` guarantees that when sample `t` exits the `la`-sample delay line at `t + la`, every sample in the boxcar is $\le r(t)$, so $g_{\text{sm}}(t + la) \le r(t)$ and peaks are strictly bounded with a linear attack ramp. Note that a single boxcar ramp has a $C^0$ slope corner at attack start/end (whereas `OutputStage`'s dual boxcar gives a smoother $C^1$ S-curve with lower distortion).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Giannoulis, Massberg & Reiss (*"Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design — A Tutorial and Analysis"*, JAES 2012) and Linkwitz (*"Active Crossover Networks for Noncoincident Drivers"*, JAES 1976).
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Parallel multiband dry/wet phase cancellation (`fx_compressor.h:207-215`)**: Best-practice multiband compressors (and parallel EQs) derive `dry` from the sum of the un-gained crossover bands (`sig[0] + sig[1] + sig[2]`, delayed by `lookAhead` in Limit mode) so `mix = 0..100%` is strictly phase-coherent and flat.
  2. **$O(W)$ limiter loops (`fx_compressor.h:322-333`)**: Round 13 M1 eliminated `% cap` (dropping Key 61 `Pentatonic Step Pluck` from 60.5% to 36.9% `[measured: docs/MPC.md:350]`), yet `Limiter::process` still scans `la + 1` floats twice per sample per band instead of using $O(1)$ van Herk / Gil-Werman (1992/1993) + $O(1)$ integer running sum as `OutputStage` already does!
  3. **4-biquad all-pass waste (`fx_compressor.h:140`)**: Computing `LR4_LP(f2) + LR4_HP(f2)` runs **4 biquads** per channel when a single 2nd-order Butterworth all-pass (`AP2`, 1 biquad) has the exact same transfer function!

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Deep notches in parallel multiband compression (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:133-145, 214-215]`)**: Mixing `mix = 50%` on multiband cancels frequencies around `crossLow` and `crossHigh`.
- **Crossover coefficient clicks (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:57-77]`)**: DF2T biquads step abruptly on `crossLow`/`crossHigh` modulation; no smoothing on `mix`, `makeup`, or thresholds.
- **Mode-switch stale buffer (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:208]`)**: Switching `mode` between Compress (latency 0) and Limit (latency `lookAhead`) leaves stale audio in `lim`/`dry`, and triggers `IntDelay::clear()` glitch on aligned buses.
- **Multiband Limit overshoot**: Summing 3 independently limited bands (each with makeup gain) can exceed `thrU` due to band addition and LR4 transient ringing.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-sample `libm` and divisions**:
  - *Compress mode*: Up to **6 `std::pow` (or `fastmath::pow`) + 6 float divisions** `(x2 + e*N)/(N+1)` per sample (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:162-173]`). Hoisting `aA = 1/(Na+1)`, `aR = 1/(Nr+1)` eliminates all 6 divisions (replacing with `e + (x2 - e)*a`).
  - *Serum mode*: Calls **`std::log10` per band per sample** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:182]`) even when `fast == true` (tier `low`)!
  - *Limit mode*: Runs `la + 1` NEON min-scan + `la + 1` scalar sum + **2 divisions** (`1/relN`, `sum/(la+1)`) per band per sample (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:197, 322-333]`). At 48 kHz 3-band Limit, that is ~438 array reads + 219 adds + 6 divs per sample (`0.0051 ms` per 128-frame block for 1 band on Mac `[measured: docs/MPC.md:338]`, ~20–30% of block on Key 61).
- **Per-control-period waste**: `set()` unconditionally calls `Biquad::design` 8 times (8 `cos`, 8 `sin`, 8 `pow`, 40 divs) every 32 samples (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_compressor.h:62-77]`) even when `crossLow`/`crossHigh` are static!
- **Memory**: Small (~12 KB per `Compressor` instance — fits in L1D).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix parallel multiband phase cancellation** by using `sig[0] + sig[1] + sig[2]` (delayed by `lookAhead` in Limit mode) as the dry signal when `bands < 3`, and replace `ap[c][0..3]` (4 biquads) with 1 `AP2` biquad. *Gain:* Large (quality) + Small (CPU). *Risk:* Low–Medium (fixes comb notches on multiband parallel presets). *Proving measurement:* Multiband `mix = 50%`, `ratio = 0%` noise frequency response (verify 0 dB flat instead of deep notches).
2. **Port `OutputStage`'s $O(1)$ van Herk + integer boxcar to `Compressor::Limiter`, hoist `1/(N+1)` and `1/(la+1)` per block, use `fastmath::log2` in Serum mode on tier `low`, and cache `set()` crossover coefficients**. *Gain:* Large (CPU on Limit/Serum/Multiband presets). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low --sections` on `Pentatonic Step Pluck`, `Karplus Harp`, `Detonation Tail`, and Serum-mode multiband presets.

### Module 6: Distortion (All 14 Types)

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:667-749`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:264-294`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- Single-rate (1× host `sampleRate`) stereo waveshaper with optional Pre (`order == 1`) or Post (`order == 2`) 12 dB Cytomic SVF (`Svf`, blend `0..2` = LP/BP/HP, keytracked to `f.lastNote`), linear dry/wet mix, and a 10 Hz 1-pole DC blocker on asymmetric types (`6, 7, 10, 11, 13`).
- **All 14 types (`fx_effects.h:711-734`)**: `0` Soft clip (`tanh(v)`), `1` Hard clip (`clamp(v, -1, 1)`), `2` Linear fold (triangle wavefold), `3` Sine fold (`sin(2*pi*fract(0.5 - 0.25*v))`), `4` Bit crush (`round(x/q)*q`), `5` Down sample (per-channel S&H), `6` Tube (`tanh(v+0.3) - tanh(0.3)`), `7` Diode 1 (asymmetric exp), `8` Diode 2 (`exp(v-) - exp(-v+)`), `9` Zero-Square (`tanh(32*v)`), `10` Asym (`tanh(v+) + max(v-, -0.5)`), `11` Rectify (`|clamp(v, -1, 1)|`), **`12` X-Shaper (UNIMPLEMENTED: `clamp(v, -1, 1)`)**, **`13` X-Shaper Asym (UNIMPLEMENTED: falls into `default:` `clamp(v, -1, 1)`)**.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Harmonic expansion & aliasing**: Memoryless nonlinearities $y = f(g x)$ generate harmonics well beyond $f_s/2$. For smooth `tanh(g x)` (types 0, 6), bandwidth scales roughly linearly with drive $g$; for slope-discontinuous shapes (Hard clip type 1, Linear fold type 2, Asym type 10, Rectify type 11), harmonics decay only as $O(1/k^2)$; for near-step shapes (Zero-Square `tanh(32 g x)` type 9), harmonics decay as $O(1/k)$ out to $k \sim 32 g$! Running all 14 shapes at **1× sample rate with zero oversampling and zero ADAA** folds all harmonics above 22.05 kHz straight back into the audible band as harsh, inharmonic metallic hash — precisely the artifact `docs/engine-spec/06-effects.md:10` warned must be oversampled!
- **Asymmetric DC build-up**: Types 6, 7, 10, 11, and 13 produce even harmonics and a non-zero DC component proportional to input amplitude; the 10 Hz 1-pole DC blocker (`fx_effects.h:735-741`) is acoustically necessary, though placing it *before* a Post filter (as done here) is good practice so the Post filter doesn't see DC offset.
- **Level compensation**: Drive spans `-30 .. +30 dB` with **zero automatic output compensation**. Types 0, 1, 6, 9 saturate at `±1.0`, types 7 and 10 saturate asymmetrically at `+1.0 / -0.5` (-3.5 dB asymmetric ceiling), and type 11 (Rectify + DC block) saturates at `±0.5` (-6 dB), so switching distortion types or sweeping drive causes dramatic loudness jumps (up to +20 dB on quiet signals).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Nonlinear distortion effects require either 2×–4× oversampling with a steep halfband IIR/FIR decimator (e.g., Laurent de Soras, *HIIR*, 2005) or **Antiderivative Antialiasing (ADAA)** — Parker, Zavalishin & Le Bivic (*"Reducing the Aliasing of Nonlinear Waveshaping Using Continuous-Time Convolution"*, DAFx 2016) and Bilbao, Esqueda, Parker & Välimäki (*"Antiderivative Antialiasing for Memoryless Nonlinearities"*, IEEE SPL 2017) — which provides 20–40 dB alias reduction at 1×–2× rate using closed-form first antiderivatives $F_1(x) = \int f(x)\,dx$ (trivial for Hard Clip, Soft Clip $\ln(\cosh x)$, Linear Fold, Diode exp, Asym, and Rectify, and combinable with 2× oversampling for Sine Fold and Zero-Square!).
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Zero anti-aliasing** across all nonlinear types (worse than the engine's own wavetable warps, which oversample 4×/2×!).
  2. **Missing X-Shaper types 12 & 13** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:732-733]`): Completely unimplemented stubs despite `06-effects.md:78` specifying 513-point curve morphing.
  3. **Severe per-sample `libm` waste** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:706-709, 738]`): Recomputes `pow(10, dExp)` + `v / d` division and `exp(-2*pi*10/sr)` **every single sample**, plus `Svf::process` divides every sample!

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Severe aliasing** on types 0–3 and 6–11 at 1× rate (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:675-734]`).
- **Broken X-Shaper / X-Shaper Asym** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:732-733]`): Both output plain hard clip instead of drawn curves.
- **Zipper noise**: `driveDb`, `mix`, `cutoff`, `resonance`, `blend` all step every 32 samples (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:677-690]`).
- **Stale state & denormals**: `reset()` (`fx_effects.h:673`) fails to reset `dc[2]` and `holdPhase[2]`, and `dc[c]` has no `flushDenormal()` (`line 739`).
- **Filter resonance mismatch**: `fx_effects.h:679` uses linear `k = 2 - 1.9*r` ($Q \in [0.5, 10]$) instead of `06-effects.md:81`'s cubic $Q = 0.5 + 15.5 r^3$ ($Q \in [0.5, 16]$).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Per-sample waste**: Currently computes `1× pow10 + 1× div + (1× exp if asym) + (1× div if Pre/Post SVF)` **per sample per channel** on top of the waveshaper itself (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_effects.h:696, 706, 709, 738]`). All four are block constants! (Noted as F18 in `docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:400`.)
- **Zero NEON**: Runs L then R sequentially; hoisting constants and running L+R in 2-lane NEON (or 4-sample NEON for memoryless waveshapers!) is straightforward. Memory footprint is tiny (< 128 bytes).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Add 1st-order ADAA (at 2× HIIR oversampling on medium/high, 1× ADAA or 2× HIIR on tier `low`) for nonlinear types 0–3, 6–11** (keeping Bit Crush 4 and Down Sample 5 at 1× as intentional lo-fi effects), and implement 513-point curve morphing for types 12–13 from `f.content`. *Gain:* Large (quality). *Risk:* Medium (cleaner high-register tone on distorted presets). *Proving measurement:* Aliasing FFT sweep (keys 48–96, 44.1 kHz, +18 dB drive) before vs after; X-Shaper transfer-curve test.
2. **Hoist `d`, `1/d`, `dcAlpha`, and `Svf::coefficients(cg, k)` out of the sample loop, ramp `drive`/`mix` across `kControlPeriod`, and zero `dc[]`/`holdPhase[]` in `reset()` with `flushDenormal`**. *Gain:* Medium (CPU + quality/correctness). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Distortion microbenchmark on Key 61; reset-determinism test; drive-sweep zipper FFT.

### Module 7: EQ (Both Laws) and the FX Filter

**Files:** `core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:297-408`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Equalizer (`fx_eq.h:14-182`)**: 4 bands in series (`low`, `mid`, optional `mid2`, `high`), supporting two filter laws:
  - *Law 0 (Vital SVF, `fx_eq.h:65-77, 114-161`)*: Cytomic/ZDF state-variable filter with `Q = 0.5 + 15.5 r^3`, `g = tan(pi * min(hz/sr, 0.499))`, combining `wh*hp + wb*bp + wl*lp` for shelves, bell, notch, HP, LP.
  - *Law 1 (Serum RBJ, `fx_eq.h:79-105, 162-174`)*: RBJ Audio EQ Cookbook DF2T biquads (`Q = 0.4 + 1.7417 r^3` shelves, `0.1 + 18.9053 r^4` peak/notch/LP/HP), linearly interpolating all 5 coefficients `(b0, b1, b2, a1, a2)` across `(t0, period)`.
- **FX Filter (`fx_modules.cpp:345-408`)**: Wraps `own::FilterUnit` (sharing all classic and `core/filters-serum` models audited in Part 1), adding `keytrack` (to `f.lastNote`), stereo cutoff `pan` (`±12 st`), `clean_mode`, and linear dry/wet mix (`wetGain`) in `FilterModule::run`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Nyquist frequency cramping**: Both bilinear TPT SVF ($g = \tan(\pi f_c / f_s)$) and the bilinear RBJ cookbook map $s = \infty$ to $z = -1$ (Nyquist). Above ~$0.25 f_s$ (~11 kHz at 44.1 kHz), this warps the upper skirt of high peaking bells and high shelves toward zero bandwidth (narrowing Q and forcing magnitude to 0 dB at Nyquist for bells, unlike analog EQs). Moreover, clamping $f_c / f_s \le 0.499$ (`fx_eq.h:140`) produces $g = \tan(0.499\pi) = 318.31$ and $g^2 = 1.013 \times 10^5$, wiping out ~17 bits of float32 mantissa precision in `v1` and `2*v1 - ic1`!
- **DF2T vs TPT under modulation**: Interpolating DF2T biquad coefficients `(b0..a2)` linearly across 32 samples (Law 1) preserves pole stability because the 2nd-order stability triangle is convex, **but** DF2T's state variables `(z1, z2)` undergo transient energy spikes on rapid high-Q cutoff sweeps, whereas TPT SVF (Law 0) is inherently energy-preserving and click-free under modulation — ironically, Law 0 is the one that *omits* coefficient smoothing!

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Analog-matched biquads / Orfanidis (*"Digital Parametric Equalizer Design with Prescribed Nyquist-Frequency Gain"*, JAES 1997) and Vicanek (*"Matched Second Order Digital Filters"*, 2016) eliminate Nyquist cramping at identical biquad CPU cost; Zavalishin's TPT SVF (2012) with hoisted `a1 = 1/(1 + g*(R + g))` and per-sample `g` ramping is best practice for modulated EQs.
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Per-sample division in EQ Law 0** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h:155]`): Computes `/ (1 + g*R + g*g)` every sample per band per channel (up to **8 float divisions per stereo sample**!) even though `g` and `R` are constant for the entire block.
  2. **Missing smoothing in EQ Law 0 & FX Filter mix** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h:65-77]`, `[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:399-400]`): Law 0 steps all parameters every 32 samples; `FilterModule::run` steps `wetGain` every 32 samples.
  3. **No caching or 0 dB band bypass** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h:32-59]`): Recomputes all 4 bands' trig/pow every 32 samples and filters audio through 0 dB inactive bell/shelf bands.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **Zipper noise** on modulated EQ Law 0 cutoff/gain/Q (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h:65-77]`) and modulated `fxfilter.mix` (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:399-400]`).
- **Float32 precision loss near Nyquist** (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_eq.h:140]`): `hz / sr = 0.499` -> `g = 318.3` -> `g^2 > 1e5` in Law 0 `svfProcess`.
- **Nyquist cramping** above 10 kHz @ 44.1 kHz in both EQ laws (`fx_eq.h:140, 167`).
- **FX Filter inherits Part 1 `FilterUnit` risks**: All `core/filters-serum` and classic filter issues documented in Part 1 (such as `gSafe <= 0.95` capping Moog/EMS/Diode ladders at 10.67 kHz @ 44.1 kHz, `combFeedbackLP_` cross-corruption, linear-Hz 5.5 ms smoothing, and ~180 KB/channel = ~360 KB delay allocation per `FilterUnit`) apply directly to `FilterModule` in Main, Bus 1, Bus 2, and any FX Filter pool slot.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **EQ**: Measured at **1.3 to 1.4 %** of the Key 61 block `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:350]` (scalar, 3–4 SVF divisions/sample/ch + 4× `pow/tan/2*sqrt` every 32 samples; hoisting `invDen` saves ~0.2% and skipping 0 dB bell bands saves ~0.4–0.5% `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:403-404]`). Law 1 runs `prevCo + (curCo - prevCo) * t` for 5 coeffs × 4 bands every sample even when coefficients are completely unchanged (`fx_eq.h:87-89`).
- **FX Filter**: `FilterModule::configure` calls `2× std::exp2` every 32 samples when `pan != 0` (`fx_modules.cpp:374-375`) and `semitonesToRatio` when `keytrack != 0` (`line 370`). Memory: 3× `FilterUnit` across Main/Bus 1/Bus 2 = **~1.1 MB** unconditionally allocated.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Hoist `invDen = 1 / (1 + g*(R + g))` in `Equalizer::designSvf()`, cache all 4 band designs, skip 0 dB bell/shelf bands, fast-path Law 1 when `prevCo == curCo`, and vectorize L+R stereo pair in NEON**. *Gain:* Medium (CPU: ~0.6–0.8% on Key 61). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config low --sections` on EQ presets.
2. **Add per-period linear parameter ramping to EQ Law 0 and `FilterModule::wetGain`, and clamp `hz <= 0.48 * sr` (or add Vicanek/Orfanidis de-cramped high bells/shelves)**. *Gain:* Medium (quality). *Risk:* Low (ramping) / Medium (de-cramping alters >12 kHz curve). *Proving measurement:* Sweep zipper FFT on EQ Law 0 cutoff and `fxfilter.mix`; 16 kHz bell Q plot at 44.1 kHz.

### Module 8: Hyper/Dimension, Utility, Bode, Convolve, and the Three Splitters

**Files:** `core/fx-serum-a/include/synthyboy/fx/{dsp_utils,utility,splitter_lmh}.h`, `core/fx-serum-a/src/{hyper_dimension,utility,splitter_lh,splitter_lmh,splitter_ms,stereo_splitter}.cpp`, `core/fx-serum-b/{bode,convolve,fft}.{h,cpp}`, `core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:554-1095`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Hyper/Dimension (`hyper_dimension.cpp:103-235`)**: Hyper sums input to mono (`0.5*(L+R)`), feeds up to 7 independent delay lines (`12 ms ± spread`) modulated by sine LFOs with staggered rate ratios (`0.85..1.30`), reads cubic, pans across L/R with constant-power `cos/sin`, normalizes by `1/sqrt(unison)`, and linearly mixes with stereo input. Dimension writes `monoIn` into 4 more delay lines (`5–40 ms` scaled by `dimSize`), applies slow 0.25 Hz ±12% AM, combines `(+tap1 - tap2)*0.5` on L and `(-tap3 + tap4)*0.5` on R, and adds `dimMix` on top.
- **Utility (`utility.cpp:59-194`, `utility.h:51-75`)**: Optional 10 Hz DC blocker (not exposed by `UtilityModule`), L/R polarity invert, 2nd-order Butterworth HPF (`> 20.5 Hz`) and LPF (`< 19990 Hz`), **LR4** mono-bass crossover (`8 biquads`), M/S width (`side * width`, `0..200%`), linear stereo balance pan, linear dry/wet mix.
- **Bode (`bode.cpp:39-259`)**: 129-tap Blackman-windowed odd-tap anti-symmetric FIR Hilbert transformer (`kHilbertLatency = 64` samples, dry aligned by 64-sample delay), quadrature single-sideband modulation (`up = I cos - Q sin`, `down = I cos + Q sin`), `dir`/`width` L/R sideband blend, linear-interpolated feedback delay (`0.1 ms .. 2 s`) with dual-rate blur LFOs (1.3 / 1.9 Hz) and cubic `softLimit` (`x - 4/27 x^3`, hard-capped at `±1.0` for `|x| >= 1.5`).
- **Convolve (`convolve.cpp:257-674`, `fft.cpp:53-131`, `fx_modules.cpp:725-1094`)**: Zero-latency uniform partitioned convolution: 128-tap time-domain FIR head + uniform 128-sample / 256-pt radix-2 complex FFT overlap-add tail (up to `524,288` frames = 4,095 partitions), rebuilt asynchronously on `FxWorker` (`sb-fx-job`) on IR shape changes (size via 17-tap Hann-sinc resample, attack, decay, 3-pole time-varying damp, cepstral minimum-phase `phi_min`, peak norm + IR gain), followed by live 1-pole tone tilt (or Serum 1-pole LP/HP) and integer pre-delay (`0..2 s`), equal-power mixed by `ConvolveModule`.
- **Three Splitters (`splitter_lh.cpp`, `splitter_lmh.cpp`, `splitter_ms.cpp`, `dsp_utils.h:156-302`)**:
  - `SplitterLH`: LR4 LP/HP split (`4 biquads/ch`).
  - `SplitterLMH`: Stage 1 LR4 split at `f1`; Low band passed through `LinkwitzRiley4AP` (`ap2L_`/`ap2R_`) at `f2`; Rest split at `f2` by Stage 2 LR4.
  - `SplitterMS`: Lossless matrix `midL = midR = 0.5*(L+R)`, `sideL = 0.5*(L-R)`, `sideR = -sideL`, merged via `L = midL + sideL`, `R = midR + sideR`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Haas effect & stereo width (`Hyper/Dimension`, `Utility`)**: Delays in the 5–40 ms range (Dimension) exploit the Haas / precedence effect to create apparent room width without distinct echoes. However, because Dimension's L and R tap delays are distinct (`5–17.5 ms` vs `12.5–39.5 ms`), summing to mono yields `0.5*(tap1 - tap2 - tap3 + tap4)`, introducing comb coloration in mono (whereas pure anti-phase Side-channel Haas cancels cleanly to zero in mono). `Utility`'s M/S width (`L = M + w*S, R = M - w*S`) is strictly mono-compatible (Mid is completely untouched on mono sum) and its LR4 mono-bass cleanly zeros Side below `monoBassFreq` while preserving flat magnitude reconstruction (`LP4 + HP4 = AP2`).
- **Hilbert transformer bandwidth (`Bode`)**: A 129-tap Blackman-windowed ideal Hilbert FIR ($h[k] = \frac{2}{\pi k} w[k]$ for odd $k$) has a main-lobe half-width of $\Delta f \approx 3 f_s / 128 \approx 1034\text{ Hz}$ at 44.1 kHz. Consequently, the quadrature magnitude $|H_Q(f)|$ rolls off steeply below ~800 Hz (and above ~21.2 kHz), degrading unwanted-sideband rejection from >70 dB in the mid-band to poor/AM-like leakage across bass and low-mid fundamentals (40–400 Hz).
- **Uniform vs non-uniform partitioned convolution (`Convolve`)**: With uniform partition size $B = 128$ and IR length $L$, the per-sample complex MAC count scales linearly as $O(L / B) \times 2B / B = O(L / 64)$. For a 4 s stereo IR at 48 kHz ($L = 192,000$, $P = 1,499$ partitions), uniform partitioning requires ~**24,000 float MACs/sample**; Gardner's (1995) non-uniform multi-rate schedule (e.g., $B = 128, 512, 2048, 8192$) cuts MACs by **~15×–25×** while keeping **exact zero latency**!
- **3-band LR4 phase-alignment math (`SplitterLMH`)**: As proven in Module 5(b), `LR4_LP(f2) + LR4_HP(f2)` equals a **2nd-order** Butterworth all-pass `AP2(f2)` ($\angle = -180^\circ$ at $f_2$), NOT a 4th-order `AP4(f2)` ($\angle = -360^\circ$ at $f_2$). Cascading **two** `setButterworthAP` biquads in `LinkwitzRiley4AP` (`dsp_utils.h:279-302`) doubles the phase shift on the Low band, putting Low and Mid+High $180^\circ$ out of phase around $f_2$ and breaking flat reconstruction whenever $f_1$ and $f_2$ lie within ~2 octaves!

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Bode**: An IIR Hilbert phase-differencing network (6th–8th order elliptic all-pass pair, e.g., Weaver 1956; Ansari 1987; Olli Niemitalo's classic 12-pole design) achieves >65–80 dB image rejection down to 15–20 Hz at ~12 multiplies/sample and 0 latency; if linear phase is strictly required, an equiripple Parks–McClellan (Remez) FIR achieves far narrower transition band than a Blackman window at the same 129-tap latency.
- **Convolve**: W. G. Gardner (*"Efficient Convolution Without Input-Output Delay"*, JAES 1995) non-uniform partitioned FFT convolution with real-packed Hermitian FFTs (`N/2 + 1` bins) and SIMD contiguous FDL (frequency-domain delay line) is universal industry best practice.
- **Splitters**: Linkwitz (1976) 3-band LR4 requires a single 2nd-order all-pass (`AP2`) on the Low band, plus inter-band latency alignment when bands host latency-bearing effects.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **`SplitterLMH` crossover cancellation bug (`[code core/fx-serum-a/include/synthyboy/fx/dsp_utils.h:279-302]`, `[code core/fx-serum-a/src/splitter_lmh.cpp:115-116]`)**: `AP4` instead of `AP2` on the Low band breaks unity sum.
- **Bode low-frequency image leakage (`[code core/fx-serum-b/bode.cpp:39-60]`)** and **clean-input clipping (`[code core/fx-serum-b/bode.cpp:25-31, 205-206]`)**: `softLimit` clips clean inputs $> 1.0$ even at `feedback = 0`!
- **Convolve hard click & tail cut-off on IR rebuild (`[code core/engine-own/fx/fx_modules.cpp:973-985]`)**: `takeOver()` swaps `active = fresh` with zero crossfade; **data race** on `m->live.tone` (`fx_modules.cpp:778, 1014`); potential audio-thread `shared_ptr` destruction if `FxWorker::submit()` fails (`line 989, 993`); integer `preDelaySamples` clicks on modulation (`convolve.cpp:581, 660`); non-Serum `convertToMinimumPhase` omits 2× zero-padding (`convolve.cpp:391`), causing cepstral time-aliasing; `m_toneStateL/R` lack denormal flush (`convolve.cpp:631, 647`).
- **Block/event-split dependence across `fx-serum-a`** (`[code core/fx-serum-a/src/hyper_dimension.cpp:121-124]`, `[code core/fx-serum-a/src/utility.cpp:102-106]`, `[code core/fx-serum-a/src/splitter_lh.cpp:96-100]`, `[code core/fx-serum-a/src/splitter_lmh.cpp:140-145]`, `[code core/fx-serum-a/src/splitter_ms.cpp:71-75]`): All parameter ramps divide by sub-block `n` rather than `kControlPeriod`.
- **Unsmoothed params**: `Utility` HPF/LPF/MonoBass biquads (`utility.cpp:79-100`), `SplitterLH`/`SplitterLMH` split frequencies, and all `Bode` parameters step abruptly without smoothing.

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Hyper/Dimension**: **11 identical mono delay buffers** (~176 KB vs 16 KB needed) + **up to 23 `sin`/`cos` + 7 divisions per sample** (`[code core/fx-serum-a/src/hyper_dimension.cpp:164-221]`), running Dimension even when `dimMix == 0`. Hoisting constants alone saves 1.8–4.4% on Key 61 `fx=all` and ~0.4% on XL `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:399]`.
- **Utility**: Cheap (`0.5 to 1.1 %` on Key 61 `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:356]`), though Mono Bass runs **8 biquads** (LR4, `utility.h:64-67`, not 4 one-poles as `CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:356` assumed).
- **Bode**: **64 integer modulos `% 129` + 2 `fmod` + 2 `floor` + 3 `% cap` + 4 double `sin`/`cos` per sample** (`[code core/fx-serum-b/bode.cpp:178-236]`), plus ~882 KB delay buffer per instance! On ARMv7 without fast `%`, 67 integer divisions per sample makes Bode prohibitively expensive.
- **Convolve**: **Uniform 128-sample partitioning** + full complex 256-pt FFT (`fft.cpp:53-131`, scalar, no real-FFT packing) + 256 scalar head FIR MACs/sample + `% m_numTailPartitions` + `2× % m_preDelayCapacity` per sample + CPU spike every 128 samples on `computeTailFftBlock()` + scattered `vector<vector<Complex>>` up to 33.5 MB + `reset()` calling `vector::assign` (`convolve.cpp:105-116`).
- **Splitters**: `0.3 %` on Key 61 `[measured: docs/release/CPU-REVIEW-FILTER-FX.md:359]`; `SplitterLMH` wastes 2 extra biquads/ch on the erroneous `AP4` instead of `AP2`.

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix `SplitterLMH` `ap2L_`/`ap2R_` from `LinkwitzRiley4AP` (`AP4`) to single-biquad `AP2`**. *Gain:* Large (quality) + Small (CPU). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* White-noise flat-sum check at `f1 = 500 Hz, f2 = 1000 Hz`.
2. **Refactor `Convolve` to non-uniform Gardner partitioning (128/512/2048/8192), Hermitian real-FFT, contiguous NEON MAC + head FIR, crossfaded `takeOver()`, atomic/value-passed `tone`, `std::fill` in `reset()`, and smoothed fractional pre-delay**. *Gain:* Large (CPU: ~10×–20× on long IRs + click/race fixes). *Risk:* Medium. *Proving measurement:* XL & Key 61 (`fx=all`) `tier_bench` on 2 s / 4 s IRs; TSan run; click test on IR shape automation.
3. **Eliminate all `% 129` / `fmod` / `% cap` in `Bode` (power-of-2 ring + mirrored Hilbert buffer, skip blur trig when `blur == 0`), move `softLimit` inside feedback only (or ±1.8 knee), and upgrade Hilbert to 8th-order IIR all-pass (or Remez FIR)**. *Gain:* Large (CPU + low-frequency SSB quality). *Risk:* Medium (IIR changes latency to 0). *Proving measurement:* 50 Hz–10 kHz SSB rejection sweep; `tier_bench` on `Bode Barber Riser`.
4. **Collapse `HyperDimension` to 1 shared mono delay line, hoist constant pan `cos`/`sin` and divisions, skip Dimension when `dimMix == 0`, and pass `(t0, period)` to `fx-serum-a` smoothers**. *Gain:* Medium–Large (CPU + RAM + block invariance). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* `tier_bench --config "low,fx=all" --load "Triple Fifth Lead"`; block-split null test.

### Module 9: Gain Staging, Voice-Count Gain, Headroom, and Master Stage

**Files:** `core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1461-1759`, `core/engine-own/dsp/master_stage.h`, `core/engine-own/dsp/util.h:122-168`, `docs/release/VOICE-COUNT-GAIN-SPEC.md`, `docs/release/OUTPUT-LEVEL.md`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **Voice-count gain (`own_engine.cpp:1463-1469, 1584-1655, 1672-1683`)**: At `controlPos == 0`, computes participation ratio $n_{\text{eff}} = (\sum a_i)^2 / (\sum a_i^2)$ over non-killing voices (`a_i = ampCur * velGain`), target $T = n_{\text{eff}}^{-\alpha}$ ($\alpha = 0.5$ default, exact `1.0f` for mono/legato or $n_{\text{eff}} \le 1$). Moves `voiceCountGainDb` toward $T$ instantaneously if `soundingAtPeriodStart == 0` (chord struck from silence), else slew-limited at **3.0 dB/ms down** and **0.15 dB/ms up**, linearly ramped across the 32-sample period and applied via NEON `simd::mulRamp` to all 4 voice buses (`mixL/R[0..3]`) *before* any effects, latency delays, or meters.
- **Bus gains & `master.volume` (`own_engine.cpp:1477-1497, 1517-1546, 1707-1721`)**: Bus 1/2 and Direct levels ramp linearly over `kControlPeriod`. `master.volume` (`-60 .. +6 dB`, `<= -60 -> 0.0f`) smooths via `LinearSmoother` (20 ms linear ramp in amplitude). `master.stereo_routing` cross-mixes `L' = L cos(phi) + R sin(phi)`, `R' = R cos(phi) + L sin(phi)` with $\phi = (1 - r)\pi/4$ (applied to Main before Direct is added).
- **Master stage (`master_stage.h:26-145`)**: Adds `direct * dg`, multiplies by `master.volume` gain `g`, and applies `outputLimit(y)`: exact identity for $|y| \le 1.8$ (+5.11 dBFS), smoothly saturating as $\text{sgn}(y)\,[1.8 + 0.2\tanh(5(|y| - 1.8))]$ toward $\pm 2.0$ (+6.02 dBFS), with NaN mapped to `+0.0f`. Vectorized 4-wide in NEON (`master::detail::lanes` + `limit4`) using bit-pattern magnitude checks.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Incoherent chord power & participation ratio**: For $N$ equal-amplitude uncorrelated voices, summed RMS scales as $\sqrt{N}$ (+6 dB for 4 notes) and coherent onset crests can reach $N$ (+12 dB; measured 9–10 dB chord-minus-note peak without scaling `[measured: docs/release/OUTPUT-LEVEL.md:56]`). Scaling by $G = n_{\text{eff}}^{-0.5}$ normalizes steady chord power to match a single note (0 dB power shift) and reduces the 4-note chord peak gap to 2.9–4.4 dB `[measured: docs/release/VOICE-COUNT-GAIN-SPEC.md:37]`, closely matching the 2–8 dB gap of stock MPC instruments `[measured: docs/release/OUTPUT-LEVEL.md:42-50]`. Using the inverse participation ratio $n_{\text{eff}} = S_1^2 / S_2$ avoids step jumps when quiet release tails decay out.
- **Master stage $C^2$ knee (`master_stage.h:26-32`)**: At $a = 1.8$, $f(1.8) = 1.8$, $f'(1.8) = 0.2 \cdot 5 \cdot \operatorname{sech}^2(0) = 1.0$, and $f''(1.8) = 0$. This is an exact $C^2$ spline-like soft knee — far cleaner than a $C^1$ quadratic/cubic knee — and leaving `[-1.8, +1.8]` strictly linear prevents premature harmonic distortion on loud resonant transients.
- **Equal-loudness & headroom conventions (`OUTPUT-LEVEL.md`)**: Owner's 2026-11-20 rule sets unity shell gain (`0 dB`), single-note velocity-127 peak target `T = -10.0 dBFS`, all 4/6/8-note chord peaks `<= -0.5 dBFS` without a limiter, `master.velocity_track = 100%` ($v^2$, ~12 dB from v64 to v127), per-profile `tierTrimDb` on Key 61, and `master.volume <= +1.0 dB` (leaving $\ge 5$ dB user headroom up to `+6 dB`). Note that `master.stereo_routing` at `0%` (mono) scales coherent center signals by $\cos(\pi/4) + \sin(\pi/4) = \sqrt{2}$ (+3.01 dB) while preserving constant power on uncorrelated stereo — a standard pan-law trade-off.

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: Polyphony-dependent power normalization ($1/\sqrt{n_{\text{eff}}}$) with fast attack / slow release slew limiting in dB (3 dB/ms down, 0.15 dB/ms up) and linear-to-`±1.8` $C^2$ safety limiting is sound, clean psychoacoustic design (cf. ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128 headroom practice).
- **Where it falls short**:
  1. **Mid-period chord onset bug on `medium`/`high` tiers (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1578-1655]`)**: Because `voiceCountGain` only updates at `controlPos == 0` while `eventGrid == false` processes mid-period note-ons immediately, a 4-note chord struck at e.g. sample 10 renders 22 samples at `G = 1.0` (+6 dB overshoot!), then arrives at the next `controlPos == 0` with `soundingAtPeriodStart == 4` (not 0!), missing the instant-step rule and taking 2 ms more to ramp down!
  2. **Pre-FX placement side-effect**: Scaling before FX means chords hit nonlinear effects (`Distortion`, `Compressor` thresholds) 6 dB softer than single notes — musically beneficial for avoiding muddy chord distortion, though users designing heavy-compression/distortion patches should be aware that chord drive drops with polyphony.
  3. **Inconsistent headroom inside the FX chain**: `Flanger` hard-clips at `±1.0` and `Bode` soft-clips at `±1.0` (even with `feedback = 0`), breaking the `±1.8` headroom contract between voices and `master::outputLimit`.

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **+6 dB chord-attack transient overshoot on mid-period note-ons** (`[code core/engine-own/own_engine.cpp:1578-1655]`): Violates block-size / event-offset invariance on `medium`/`high` tiers.
- **Unoversampled / unlinked `outputLimit` above `±1.8`** (`[code core/engine-own/dsp/master_stage.h:26-32, 54-55]`): If the user pushes `master.volume` to `+6 dB` on a loud chord, peaks > 1.8 alias at 1× rate and compress L/R independently.
- **Linear amplitude volume smoother (`LinearSmoother`)**: `master.volume` ramps linearly in amplitude rather than dB over 20 ms (fine for short de-zippering, slightly uneven on slow sweeps).
- **Spec inconsistency in `docs/release/OUTPUT-LEVEL.md`**: Summary & section A7 declare `T = -10.0 dBFS`, while section B6 still tabulates the superseded `T = -4.50 dBFS` run (`[measured: docs/release/OUTPUT-LEVEL.md:24, 62]`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **Master stage (`master_stage.h:77-134`)**: Excellent! Settled fast-path skips `LinearSmoother::next()`, stereo routing is template-hoisted outside the loop, and NEON `lanes()` + bit-pattern `limit4()` process 4 samples/iter with zero ARMv7 float-compare spills.
- **Voice-count gain (`own_engine.cpp:1628-1646, 1672-1683`)**: Computes 3 transcendentals (`pow`, `log10`, `pow`) once per 32 samples (could be 1 `log2` + 1 `exp2`) and multiplies all 4 buses (`t = 0..3`) via NEON `simd::mulRamp` even when Bus 1, Bus 2, and Direct are completely unused (`t = 1..3` are zero!).

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Trigger `voiceCountGain` recalculation whenever `handleEvent` changes `activeMask` (mid-period note-ons on `medium`/`high` tiers)**, preserving the `0 -> N` instant-step rule at any frame offset. *Gain:* Large (correctness / chord peak consistency). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* 4-note chord struck at frame 0 vs frame 10 on tier `high` (verify identical peak within 0.05 dB).
2. **Track which of the 4 voice buses (`mixL/R[0..3]`) were actually written in the period** to skip `simd::mulRamp` and `dlDirect`/`dlBusIn` on silent buses; add 1st-order ADAA to `outputLimit` above `±1.8`. *Gain:* Small (CPU + overload quality). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* Aliasing FFT of `outputLimit` at peak 3.0; `tier_bench` on Main-only presets.

### Module 10: The Output Stage (`output_stage.h`) and Plugin Shell Hand-Over

**Files:** `core/include/synthyboy/output_stage.h`, `shell-mpc/output_stage.h`, `shell-mpc/synthyboy_mpc.cpp:394-420, 2046, 3286-3312`, `shell-juce/PluginProcessor.cpp:295-325, 455-495`, `shell-clap/synthyboy_clap.cpp:187, 216, 350-393, 634`.

#### (a) Algorithm
- **OutputStage (`output_stage.h:49-478`)**: Header-only, zero-allocation, zero-division, zero-`libm`-per-sample stage shared by all shells and offline tools:
  1. Non-finite input sanitization -> 20 ms exponential smoothed make-up gain (`kMakeupGainDb = 0.0f` default; Key 61 adds preset `tierTrimDb` in `[-6, +9]` dB via `applyTierTrim()`).
  2. Stereo 3 ms look-ahead delay ring (`D = 132 @ 44.1k, 144 @ 48k, 288 @ 96k`, `kRing = 1024`).
  3. Stereo-linked sample-peak detector: `r = kCeilingSafe * recipBelow(p)` (integer seed `0x7EF311C7 - u` + 3 Newton–Raphson steps strictly from below + `1 - 2^-20` safety margin), fed into an $O(1)$ amortized streaming **van Herk / Gil-Werman** sliding maximum over $W = D + 1$ samples (`win`, `suf`, `pre`).
  4. Dual-stage program-dependent release in reduction domain ($1 - g$): fast stage (30 ms release) floored by a slow stage (300 ms release, 20 ms attack scaled by a sustain weight `s` tracking overloads within a 25 ms hold window).
  5. Downward-quantized (`2^-22`) gain smoothed by **two cascaded exact-integer boxcar moving averages** (`B1` power-of-two + `B2 = W - B1 + 1`, so `B1 + B2 - 1 = W = D + 1`), producing a $C^1$ S-shaped attack ramp ending strictly at $\le kCeiling = 0.94406088f$ (`-0.5 dBFS`), backed by a hard safety clip (0 hits across all 160 factory presets `[measured: the earlier output stage review (removed):106]`).
  6. **Bit-exact idle path**: When `idle && |a|, |b| <= kCeiling`, skips detector and gain multiply completely (NEON 4-wide `processLanes` using integer bit-pattern compares, bit-identical to scalar in IEEE and FTZ modes). Limiter defaults OFF (`kOutputLimiterDefaultOn = false`, latency 0); toggling `setLimiter()` crossfades linearly over 10 ms while keeping the detector primed.
- **Shell hand-overs**:
  - *MPC VST2 (`synthyboy_mpc.cpp:3286-3312`)*: Sets FTZ (`flushDenormalsOn`/`Restore`), calls `engine->process` -> `outputStage.process` -> `outputPeak` -> `spectrumRing`, defers `audioMasterIOChanged` off the audio thread via `latencyDirty` (`line 2046`), resets `outputStage` on `effMainsChanged`/`effStartProcess`.
  - *JUCE VST3/AU (`PluginProcessor.cpp:295-325, 455-495`)*: Overrides `reset()`, calls `engine->process` -> `outputStage.process`, checks latency in `processBlock()`.
  - *CLAP (`synthyboy_clap.cpp:187, 216, 350-393`)*: Prepares/resets `outputStage`, calls `engine->process` -> `outputStage.process`, reports latency via `ext_latency_get` + `host->request_restart`.

#### (b) Mathematics & Psychoacoustics
- **Exact brickwall ceiling & S-curve transparency**: Convolving a sliding minimum of support $W = D + 1$ with two rectangular kernels of lengths $B_1, B_2$ ($B_1 + B_2 - 1 = W$) creates a trapezoidal/piecewise-quadratic $C^1$ attack envelope whose support matches the hold window exactly. Because every tap in both moving averages sees a value $\le r(t_0)$ at sample $t_0 + D$, and integer `2^-22` accumulators have zero floating-point drift, peak bounding is mathematically airtight while minimizing low-frequency gain-modulation THD. The dual-stage release prevents both transient pumping (fast recovery after drums/plucks) and waveform-rate ripple on sustained bass/chords (slow stage holds through the 25 ms > half-cycle of 20 Hz window).
- **Sample peak vs True Peak (ITU-R BS.1770-4)**: The detector measures discrete sample peaks `max(|L|, |R|)` at 1× rate, relying on the `-0.5 dBFS` ceiling (`0.94406`) to absorb DAC reconstruction inter-sample peaks (ISPs). While `-0.5 dB` covers typical musical ISPs, heavily clipped/Nyquist-adjacent synth waveforms can produce +1 to +3 dB ISPs (Nielsen & Lund, AES 2003). Notice that `Compressor::Limiter` (`fx_compressor.h:297`) already computes a cheap 3-point quadratic midpoint estimate (`-0.125*x[n-2] + 0.75*x[n-1] + 0.375*x[n]`) that catches most ISPs at 3 multiplies/sample.
- **Toggle crossfade combing**: Crossfading linearly over 10 ms between undelayed `a` and `D`-sample delayed `da` (`output_stage.h:371-372`) briefly comb-filters the signal during the 10 ms switch (acceptable for a manual UI toggle, unavoidable when latency changes dynamically).

#### (c) Best Practice & Literature
- **Best practice**: `OutputStage` is textbook best-in-class real-time DSP — van Herk (1992) / Gil & Werman (1993) $O(1)$ morphological min/max filtering, cascaded boxcar S-curve look-ahead (Abel & Berners / standard broadcast limiters), integer drift-free running sums, divide-free Newton reciprocal, and bit-exact NEON idle passthrough. It is by far the highest-quality DSP component in Part 2.
- **Where the shell hand-over falls short**:
  1. **CLAP mono buffer aliasing (`[code shell-clap/synthyboy_clap.cpp:353]`)**: Sets `outR = (channel_count > 1) ? data32[1] : outL`, passing `left == right` into `OwnEngine::process()`. `OwnEngine::process` only checks `rightOut == nullptr` (`own_engine.cpp:1333`), so `master::stage` overwrites `outL` with the Right channel, dropping Left entirely instead of downmixing `0.5*(L+R)`!
  2. **CLAP premature sleep truncating tails (`[code shell-clap/synthyboy_clap.cpp:392]`)**: Returns `(activeVoices() > 0) ? CLAP_PROCESS_CONTINUE : CLAP_PROCESS_SLEEP`, immediately putting the plugin to sleep when the last voice ends and **cutting off all Reverb/Delay/Convolve tails and the 3 ms OutputStage look-ahead tail!**
  3. **JUCE non-RT-safe `setLatencySamples()` inside `processBlock()` (`[code shell-juce/PluginProcessor.cpp:494]`)**: Calling `AudioProcessor::setLatencySamples()` on the audio thread triggers `updateHostDisplay()` (host notification / mutexes).

#### (d) Quality Risks
- **CLAP mono corruption and tail truncation** (`[code shell-clap/synthyboy_clap.cpp:353, 392]`): Directly audible bugs for CLAP hosts (Bitwig, Reaper, Studio One).
- **JUCE audio-thread glitch on latency change** (`[code shell-juce/PluginProcessor.cpp:494]`): Toggling Bode or Compressor Limit live can stall the audio callback in JUCE hosts.
- **Inter-sample peaks above 0 dBTP when heavily limited** (`[code core/include/synthyboy/output_stage.h:52, 215]`): Sample-peak ceiling `-0.5 dBFS` does not guard against >0.5 dB DAC ISPs on clipped/aliased waveforms.
- **Note**: Earlier review items (the earlier output stage review (removed) items (g) JUCE `reset()` and (h) MPC `latencyDirty` / `audioMasterIOChanged`) are already fixed in this tree (`PluginProcessor.cpp:295-300`, `synthyboy_mpc.cpp:405, 417-419, 2046, 3295-3296`).

#### (e) Cost & Hardware Analysis
- **OutputStage cost**: **0.431 µs** (idle) and **1.313 µs** (actively limiting) per 128-frame block on Mac (`0.015–0.045%` of budget `[measured: the earlier output stage review (removed):46]`). Zero divisions, zero `libm` calls, zero allocations, bit-exact NEON idle fast path (`processLanes` uses integer `vcleq_u32` compares — zero ARMv7 VFP spills).
- **Memory**: `~22.5 KB` total (`dl[1024]`, `dr[1024]`, `win[1025]`, `suf[1025]`, `ring1[512]`, `ring2[1024]`) — fits comfortably inside Cortex-A17's 32 KB L1D cache!

#### (f) Concrete Recommendations
1. **Fix CLAP mono pointer (`outR = nullptr` when `channel_count <= 1`) and CLAP sleep condition (keep returning `CLAP_PROCESS_CONTINUE` until FX tails and `OutputStage` delay have drained below -120 dBFS)**, and **defer JUCE `setLatencySamples()` to the 20 Hz timer via an atomic `latencyDirty` flag**. *Gain:* Medium–Large (correctness & RT safety on desktop shells). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* CLAP mono-bus sum test, CLAP reverb-tail sleep test, and JUCE RT-safety check on live Bode toggle.
2. **Optionally add a 3-point quadratic (or 4× polyphase) inter-sample peak check in `OutputStage::detect`** (only when `p > 0.707 * kCeiling`, so the idle path remains bit-exact and zero-cost!). *Gain:* Small (True-Peak safety). *Risk:* Low. *Proving measurement:* ITU-R BS.1770-4 True-Peak sweep across all 160 presets with `LIMIT = ON`.

## What to Watch at the Finest Level

1. **Never put `AP4` where `LR4` sums to `AP2`**: `LR4_LP + LR4_HP` is a **2nd-order** all-pass (`SplitterLMH` used `AP4`, `Compressor` wasted 4 biquads).
2. **Never run nonlinear waveshapers at 1× without ADAA or oversampling**: all 14 `Distortion` types and `outputLimit` above 1.8 alias at 1×.
3. **Never hard-clip inside an effect at ±1.0 when engine headroom is ±1.8**: `Flanger` (`clamp(-1, 1)`) and `Bode` (`softLimit` even at `feedback = 0`) clip clean signals.
4. **Never divide or call `libm` on block constants inside sample loops**: seen in `Distortion` (`pow10`, `exp`, `Svf`), `Reverb` Vintage (16 `sin` + 18 `exp` + 18 divs), `EQ` Law 0 (8 divs), `Delay` HQ (112 divs!), `HyperDimension` (23 `sin`/`cos` + 7 divs), `Flanger`/`Phaser` (`rate/sr`).
5. **Never use `% non_pow2` or `fmod` in ARMv7 hot loops**: `Bode` runs 64× `% 129` + 2× `fmod` per sample (`__aeabi_idivmod` software divide on Cortex-A17).
6. **Always use $O(1)$ sliding extrema & running sums for look-ahead limiters**: `OutputStage` does it right (van Herk + integer ring); `Compressor::Limiter` still scans $O(W)$ twice per band.
7. **Always phase-align parallel dry/wet paths and splitter bands**: `Compressor` multiband parallel mix lacks crossover phase alignment; `FxChain` splitters lack inter-band `IntDelay`.
8. **Never step modulated delay times or filter coefficients over multi-sample holds**: tier `low` Chorus (4-sample hold) and Phaser (8-sample hold) create 11 kHz / 5.5 kHz zipper; linearly interpolate instead.
9. **Never ramp parameters over sub-block `n`**: `fx-serum-a` modules and `makeupOfBus` divide ramps by `n` instead of `kControlPeriod`, breaking event-split invariance.
10. **Update polyphony gain on mid-period note-ons, not only `controlPos == 0`**: `voiceCountGain` lets up to 31 samples of +6 dB chord attack slip through on `medium`/`high`.
11. **Crossfade every state/topology swap**: instance-0 OFF bypass, `fx.order` reorder, `IntDelay` resize, `Reverb` type switch, and `Convolve` `takeOver()` all hard-click today.
12. **Never use uniform 128-sample FFT partitioning for multi-second IRs**: non-uniform Gardner partitioning + real Hermitian FFT cuts `Convolve` CPU by >10×.
13. **Never allocate all mutually exclusive algorithm buffers simultaneously**: 3× `Reverb` (~14 MB) and 3× `Delay` (~6 MB) bloat RSS and startup `prepare_fx` (73.6 ms).
14. **Never duplicate identical mono delay lines**: `HyperDimension` writes `monoIn` into 11 separate buffers instead of 1 shared ring.
15. **Never use `readCubic` on exact integer constant delays**: `Reverb` runs dozens of 4-point cubic interpolations per sample on fixed integer all-pass and plate taps.
16. **Never hardcode `44100.0f` for time durations or scale all-passes by power-of-two rate steps**: breaks `FxModule` flush hold at 96 kHz and causes 18% RT60 shift in Type 0 Reverb at 48 kHz.
17. **Guard damped-shelf scaling against sign inversion**: Nitrous Space `1 - (1 - ahRaw) * 1.45` goes negative below `-9.9 dB`.
18. **Flush denormals in every recursive filter state and clear every state in `reset()`**: Phaser `bpA`/`bpB`, Distortion `dc`, and Convolve `m_toneState` lack denormal flushes; Distortion `reset()` forgets `dc`/`holdPhase`.
19. **Never share non-atomic state with worker threads or call `vector::assign` in RT `reset()`**: `ConvolveModule::runJob` races on `live.tone`; `Convolve::reset()` calls `assign`.
20. **Guard shell audio hand-overs for mono aliasing, FX tails, and RT-safe latency updates**: CLAP aliases `outR = outL` and sleeps on `activeVoices == 0`; JUCE calls `setLatencySamples` in `processBlock`.
21. **Skip work for neutral/muted paths**: skip 0 dB EQ bands, muted splitter band racks, `dimMix == 0` Dimension taps, `blur == 0` Bode trig, `erLevel == 0` Reverb ER taps, and empty voice buses in `voiceCountGain`.
